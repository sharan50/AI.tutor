Papers and grade ranges: deterministic, no model call

Practice papers and the provisional grade range are computed, not generated. Neither involves a model call, and that single property is what lets them inherit the item bank's validation instead of needing a verification story of their own.

ComponentInputMethodWhy it is not a model call
Paper assemblerThe paper's structural specification, plus live items with mark, command-word and difficulty metadata.Constraint satisfaction over the bank: hit the mark total, the coverage profile, the command-word mix and the difficulty distribution, excluding items the learner has seen recently.A generated paper would need every item verified afresh. An assembled paper contains only items that are already validated, so the paper's correctness is a property of the bank. T-PAP-01 checks the assembly, not the content.
Grade rangeMasteryState: unaided-check results on validated items.An arithmetic mapping from performance to a band, with an evidence-sufficiency gate below which nothing is shown.Asking a model to estimate a grade would produce a fluent, unfalsifiable number. An arithmetic mapping can be calibrated against real outcomes by T-CAL-01, and can be shown to be wrong.

The consequence worth stating: neither feature adds a cost line in 10, because neither consumes vendor tokens. They are close to free at the margin, which is unusual for the two features most visible to a paying parent.
