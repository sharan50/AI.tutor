The verifier runs on a different pinned model from the generator, and check (d) runs on no model at all. A verifier that shares the generator's weights shares its errors, and the correlated-failure mode is exactly the one that produces a confident wrong answer that passes its own check. What it makes harder: two vendor relationships to qualify instead of one, two regression surfaces under D22, and a second cost line per turn.

The gate-fire rate: the proportion of generated turns the verifier blocks. Not known and not guessable. It sets the cost of P2 twice over, once in wasted generation and once in learner experience, since every blocked turn is a question a free chatbot would have answered fluently. It is measured from the first frozen-battery run at M2 in 11 Roadmap, and it is the first number to look at when that run completes.
