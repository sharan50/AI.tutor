Kill switch mechanics

The service levels are in 04. This is what makes them achievable, and the fifteen-minute figure is a consequence of exactly one design choice repeated in several places: nothing derived from a persona is cached for longer than sixty seconds, and nothing derived from a persona is ever baked into an artefact.

Where persona data could persistBoundHow it is enforced
The registry flag itself.Immediate.Single write. No deployment.
Application cache of the preset.60 seconds.Hard TTL, set in one place, asserted by a test.
In-flight session, already holding a rendered card.Next turn, ceiling 16 minutes.The flag is re-read at every turn, not at session start. Falls back to fallback_archetype_id.
CDN, catalogue, marketing, app store metadata.24 hours.Scripted purge, rehearsed rather than assembled on the day.
Primary stores: style object, card, name, likeness assets, and every preset_snapshot referencing the preset.7 days.The renderer is the only path by which a card is produced, and the snapshot is the only place a produced vector is retained. Both are deleted, or the promise is not kept.
Backups.35 days.Retention cycle. Stated in the licence rather than omitted.
Model weights.Not applicable.D8. No LoRAs, no fine-tunes, no adapters carrying persona style. There is nothing to retrain, which is the only reason any of the above is credible.

The kill switch is rehearsed quarterly against a live archetype and the elapsed time is recorded. An untested switch is a claim, and this one is a contractual commitment to a creator. The rehearsal is cheap because D11 guarantees the product keeps running with the preset disabled.
