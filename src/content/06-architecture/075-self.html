Rendering the self-tutor

The self-tutor adds steps after the gate and changes nothing before it. The likeness is applied only to text that has already passed, so a renderer can fail to say a sentence but can never change one. Everything in this section follows from keeping the likeness downstream of the verifier (D64).

Deletion

Deletion reuses the kill switch's mechanics rather than inventing new ones (above). The promise is that a learner who deletes their self-tutor stops hearing it at the next turn and is out of every primary store within seven days (D63).

The same deletion runs when the account closes, and after twelve months without a self-tutor session.

What it leans on

  • D8's reasoning, applied to learners. Weights cannot be cleanly revoked, so none are trained on a face or a voice (D65). That is the only reason the seven-day line is credible.
  • D24. The speech and animation vendor is admitted on the same zero-retention terms as the model vendors (below).
  • The gate. Because the renderers sit after it, T-STY-03's property extends to the likeness: neither style nor face nor voice can change what is true. A rendering failure costs the clip, never the turn.
  • Degradation, as D11 requires of personas. Without the vendor, the flag or a likeness, the learner gets the standard tutor and the same text. No step in the turn pipeline depends on the self-tutor.
