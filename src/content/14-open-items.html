For counsel: ten questions, deliberately unanswered

No answer, no likely answer, and no range is offered for any of these. Supplying one would be worse than leaving the question open, because a plausible guess in a repository like this one gets quoted later as though it were advice. Seven come from the brief; three (OI-8, OI-9, OI-10) were raised while writing this design and are new.

#QuestionUnblocksCost of getting it wrong
OI-1Does AQA's AI clause reach runtime retrieval, or only training?Whether any board-derived material could ever sit in a retrieval index, and therefore whether the clean room is a strategy or merely the current one.A product built on an index that has to be destroyed, and a claim we would struggle to defend.
OI-2Do the other boards' positions differ, and does any board licence for AI use at any price?Board alignment (D19), and whether a licensed route exists at all.Building the clean room at full cost when a licence was available, or assuming a licence exists when none does.
OI-3What exact wording may be used to state which specification a course follows, without implying endorsement? We are proceeding ahead of this answer under decision D34 and the convention in 04, because a product that will not name the examination it prepares for cannot be sold. This is the only question in this register we have not waited for.Every piece of marketing copy, and the product's own course naming. Already in use.A trade mark complaint that removes the product from app stores faster than it could be defended. Contained by centralised naming strings and a 24-hour rollback, not eliminated. First in the counsel instruction.
OI-4Is a one-to-one tutor with no inter-user sharing outside the Online Safety Act user-to-user duties, and precisely which feature changes that?The whole regulatory posture, and the trigger list in 05.Verified Penalties to the higher of £18m or 10% of qualifying worldwide revenue, plus business disruption reaching ISPs, app stores and payment providers.
OI-5What age assurance method would satisfy the ICO for a service whose entire user base is children, at a cost compatible with a consumer price point?The Route A launch, and the unit economics, since verification cost subtracts directly from the acquisition budget.Verified The Reddit outcome: £14.47m, specifically for inadequate age assurance.
OI-6What is the minimum viable creator licence for name and likeness only, and what does the exemplar-conditioning upgrade under D6 add to it?The first creator signature, and the D6 contingency.A licence too thin to permit what the product does, or thick enough to require a negotiation per creator, which destroys the D5 economics.
OI-7Which entity contracts with the parent, and what does that do to consumer jurisdiction, UK representative obligations and VAT?Incorporation, the payment stack, the terms of business, and the international transfer position.A consumer contract unenforceable in the market it was written for, and a tax position discovered late.
OI-8New. Is the default of no parental transcript access (D16) defensible where the learner is under 13, and does the answer differ between Route A and Route B?The parent reporting surface and the parent-facing terms.A Children's code finding against the product's central reporting feature, and a rebuild of the parent surface.
OI-9New. Under what licence may the DfE-published subject content be used as the source for commercial item authoring, and does that use carry conditions?The entire item bank. The clean room depends on there being a lawful source to write from.The clean-room policy has no permitted source, and every item in the bank inherits the defect.
OI-11New. If a provisional grade range is shown to a teacher in a form that could inform a Foundation or Higher tier entry decision, does Article 22 become engaged, and what is then required?Any teacher-facing view of the range, which is a predictable Route B feature request.Automated decision-making with a significant effect on a child, in a product sold to the school that makes the decision. Tier entry caps the grade a child can be awarded.
OI-10New. Is Article 6(1)(b) the correct basis for processing a child's data under a contract held by their parent, or is legitimate interests sounder? How does the Article 8 threshold interact?The privacy notice, the DPIA, and the consent architecture.The second limb of the Reddit finding: profiling children without a lawful basis.

Open assumptions: reasoned, not verified

Every one of these shapes the plan and none has been confirmed. Nine are carried from the brief; nine were introduced by this design and are marked new. They are grouped by what they would break.

The pattern in this list

Worth noticing, because it shapes what M1 should be: seven of the eighteen assumptions are resolvable by reading or by asking, not by building or by advice. OA-2 is a few hours with three published policies. OA-5 is reading five sets of terms. OA-8 is one notified text. OA-13 is a tax reference. OA-16 is an hour with the Children's code. OA-17 is thirty phone calls. OA-10 is a landing page.

Those seven include the most load-bearing assumption in the repository and the one that decides whether Route B has a market. None of them requires a lawyer, an engineer or a product. They are in M1 for that reason, and a plan that starts by building instead is choosing to pay the highest available price for the same information.
