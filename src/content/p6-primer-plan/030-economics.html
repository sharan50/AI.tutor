Economics

Almost every figure the Primer's business needs is unknown, and none is filled in here; the tablet adds a second set, for making and supporting hardware, as unknown as the first. The table names what would measure each one, and when. That is the discipline the Tutor's economics follows (10), applied to an idea at an earlier stage.

For the Tutor, 10 derives the variable that dominates the economics. For the Primer it cannot yet be derived. There are three candidates: the price a parent will pay (OA-22, OA-54); the cost of the reviewed catalogue, explainers included, which grows with every module and does not get cheaper as models improve (RP5); and the tablet's cost to make, certify, send and support, which recurs with every child (RP7). If the catalogue dominates, the Primer's economics look more like a publisher's than a software company's; if the tablet does, more like a device maker's.
