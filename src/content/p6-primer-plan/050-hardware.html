Making the tablet

The tablet is designed early and made late. PM4 runs the built Primer on a stand-in tablet locked to the Primer; the dedicated tablet is designed alongside PM4 and made only once PM4's evidence is in. Making means tooling, a first batch and certification for sale in each country, the spending least recoverable if PM4 stops the Primer, so it waits, as D32's order asks. The design runs in parallel so that a good result at PM4 does not then wait for one; its cost is Unknown, and it is lost if PM4 stops the Primer.

The Primer is its own tablet, as every film shows: one per child, with no browser, no other apps and no notifications.

DecisionD105, the owner's, 31 October 2026

The stand-in, for PM4. A tablet already on sale, one per pilot child, locked so that it runs only the Primer, with no browser, no other apps and no notifications; its lock also switches off the system's own voice assistant and wake word. It shows the screen the films show, which is software (D109), and its microphone is live only while the child holds the talk key, with the screen showing plainly that it is listening (D116); T-PRM-13 checks every path that can open the microphone, on each stand-in before any child uses it and on every software release, the stand-in's own system updates included. On PM4's stand-ins, the system's own updates are held by the device management until T-PRM-13's software check has passed on them. Its body is not the films' matte off-white tablet. Which tablet suits is Unknown, and it is chosen before PM4: one without a camera, so that T-PRM-13 runs on it in full. If none suits, the owner chooses between one whose camera is switched off and covered, which T-PRM-13 would not pass, and pilot units of the dedicated design, which would have to be ready before PM4 (P4); that choice is the owner's and has not been made. What using a stand-in in a supervised pilot requires is part of the pilot's protocol and DPIA; OI-31 asks what a tablet sold to families must meet.

The dedicated tablet, designed alongside PM4. It is designed to D105, D109 and D116: the matte off-white tablet of the films, nothing on it but the Primer, no camera, and a talk key the microphone obeys. Until counsel answers OI-31, it is designed and costed as if each regime the candidates describe applies: the security rules in the UK, radio authorisation in both countries and, in the US, children's product testing (routes, P4). T-PRM-13 inspects every hardware revision, and checks every path that can open the microphone on every software release. Who designs and makes it, and where, is deliberately not decided (ledger).

Made only after PM4's evidence. Tooling, a first batch and certification follow PM4, and PM5's purchases are of the dedicated tablet and the service. The batch is still a bet placed before PM5 shows whether parents pay at a real price, which is why it waits for PM4 at least. Whether PM5 takes orders before the batch is made, so that what parents pay is known before the batch is paid for, is not decided here; the batch's size is Unknown.

If the tablet cannot be made and sold at a price families will pay (RP7), the design has no fallback. An app on a family's own tablet is the alternative OA-54 tests against, and only the owner could choose it, by reversing D105.
