Milestones defined by evidence

The ordering rule is the Tutor's: buy information in ascending order of what it costs, and write no dates (D32, 11). Each milestone is complete when the evidence is in hand, and each names the result that would stop the Primer there. The tablet adds no milestone: PM4 tests the built Primer on a stand-in, and the dedicated tablet is designed alongside and made only after PM4's evidence (making the tablet).

PM3 is the first milestone that involves children, and it cannot start until the safeguarding policy, the research protocol and parental consent exist, and until these preconditions are met: facilitators vetted to the standard required for work with children in each country; a parent present, or the session observed by a second adult; the child's own assent, and a way to stop at any moment; a DPIA for the pilot; no camera on the child and nothing of the child recorded as audio or video, as the Primer itself keeps neither (D102, D104, D116); the Primer's rules kept by the facilitator, a safety reply shown and never spoken among them (D115); and pilot records deleted at the pilot's end unless the parent consents otherwise, kept meanwhile for a period Unknown until the policy sets it (P4). They are the conditions for a child in any Primer session, PM4's included, and in any session that makes test recordings. There a child reads a script, written and reviewed with the safeguarding specialist, that voices nothing of their own life; anything a child says of their own takes 05's path, and is cut from the recording before it becomes test material, held in the hold, only for the trained reviewer, until the review closes where it takes 05's path, and otherwise deleted at once, with no backup of the recording kept; and the recording is the one exception to recording nothing of a child. Everything before PM3 is with adults only, which is why the field research guide collects no child's data.

A demonstration for adult testers, built in days, can support PM1's conversations (D70). It is described in the MVP build guide. It is not the built Primer of PM4: it answers from no reviewed bank, no child uses it, and nothing it shows is evidence about children. It also predates the films, and stays as it is (D101): a web app for phones and computers, for adults behind a passcode; an answer typed out, with three building blocks whose clips are generated live, labelled not checked and given no sources; any question, with no topics, no parent screen and nothing kept, not even a summary; a close that offers a new question rather than "Leave it there, or keep going?" (D103); and Version C's football thread stopped as a question about the asker's own life. A tester at PM1 who sees both is told which is the design: the films, which are illustrative and made with AI, show the design and no working system, and the demonstration lets a tester try an earlier one.
