Safety, privacy, regulatory

Every user of this product is a child. That is not an edge case to be handled, it is the operating condition, and 2026 is the year the UK regulator chose to make an example of companies that treated it otherwise.

The enforcement climate, stated plainly

The Information Commissioner's Office fined Reddit £14.47m on 24 February 2026 for unlawfully processing children's personal data, specifically for inadequate age assurance and for profiling children without a lawful basis. MediaLab was fined earlier the same month. Earlier actions in the same programme: TikTok £12.7m, Snap £1.95m.

VerifiedICO enforcement, Feb 2026

On 12 March 2026 the ICO wrote to online platforms stating that self-declared age is no longer adequate where a service is likely to be accessed by children, and that platforms should use modern age assurance technologies. The ICO has separately said it will audit edtech providers.

VerifiedICO open letter, 12 Mar 2026

What this rules out

A date-of-birth field at signup is not a compliance position. It is the specific thing two companies were fined for in the month before this document was written.

The one advantage the UK has over India here

The UK has no categorical prohibition on profiling children. Under the Children's code, profiling is permissible where there is a lawful basis and the design is right: high privacy by default, profiling off unless justified, the best interests of the child as the primary consideration. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act prohibits tracking and behavioural monitoring of under-eighteens outright, and parental consent cannot waive it.

VerifiedICO Children's code; DPDP Act s.9(3)

This is the reason the adaptive engine survives in the UK and would not have survived in India as a direct-to-consumer product. It is also the reason the justification for every inference has to be written down before it is built, not after.

Age assurance

The requirement is unusual: this service is not trying to keep children out, it is built for them. What it needs to establish is which band a user is in, so that defaults, content and data handling can differ, and so that the consent route is correct.

Proposed position, requiring counsel sign-off: age assurance at account creation through a third-party provider, with the parent as account holder and payer for under-16s, and the assurance signal retained rather than the underlying identity evidence. Online Safety Act scoping

Ofcom has confirmed that a service is user-to-user where users can share chatbot-generated content with other users, or where users can create chatbots that are made available to others. The trigger for Ofcom's intervention in this area was users building virtual clones of real people.

VerifiedOfcom open letter, 8 Nov 2024

Working position: a private one-to-one tutor with no sharing between users sits outside the user-to-user duties. This is reasoned from Ofcom's published examples and has not been confirmed by counsel.

The trigger that changes this

Any feature that lets a user create a persona available to other users, or share a session, converts this service into a regulated user-to-user platform with illegal content duties, children's safety duties, risk assessments and reporting mechanisms attached. Penalties reach the higher of £18m or 10% of qualifying worldwide revenue.

The original 2025 submission had user-created personas at months 12 to 18 as a roadmap line. It is removed as a roadmap line and becomes a dated, costed decision with a compliance programme attached.

Content safety inside a tutoring session

A tutor that a child talks to alone at eleven at night will eventually be told something that is not about physics. Three requirements, none of which are optional.

  • The tutor does not counsel. It responds with warmth, does not pretend to be a friend or a person, and routes to a named source of help appropriate to the UK.
  • Defined escalation: what is surfaced to the parent account, what is not, and where the line sits, written down and visible to the parent before they buy.
  • A persona never changes this behaviour. Style governs how mathematics is taught and nothing else. The style object cannot reach safety behaviour, and that isolation is tested.

The isolation requirement is the single most important constraint the architecture places on the style engine, and it is a hard boundary rather than a preference. A style card that could influence a safety response would mean a creator's licensed persona was implicated in the handling of a child in distress.

Where the owner's risk ranking is not followed

The owner ranked a child being harmed or their data leaking fourth of four, below a rights suit, below teaching something wrong, and below nobody paying. As a ranking of what is existential and outside his control, that order is defensible.

It is not the order in which this is built. Child safety and data minimisation are built to the top standard regardless of position on that list, because they are cheap to design in and ruinous to retrofit, and because in the chosen market the regulator with an active enforcement programme pointed at this sector is the one that governs exactly this. Document 12 records the ranking as given and this exception alongside it.
