Foundation and markets

#AssumptionUnblocks / shapesCost of being wrong

OA-31New. That the US has proven more receptive than the UK to consumer education products for children. OA-35New. That US parents of students of 14 to 18 would price the Tutor against a human tutor, as OA-10 assumes UK parents will. Testable with a US landing page like OA-10's, once the US course is chosen.
