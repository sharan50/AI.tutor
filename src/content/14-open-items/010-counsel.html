For counsel: twelve questions, none answered

No answer, no likely answer, and no range is offered for any of these. All are also unacted on except OI-3, where D34 proceeds under a fixed convention because waiting would mean a product that cannot describe itself, and OI-23 to OI-27, where the self-tutor proceeds under D59's rule: it is withdrawn only if counsel's answer is a categoric no. Supplying one would be worse than leaving the question open, because a plausible guess in a repository like this one gets quoted later as though it were advice. Seven come from the brief; five (OI-8 to OI-12) were raised while writing the Tutor's design; eight (OI-13 to OI-20) were raised by the Primer and Foundations; two (OI-21 and OI-22) by the Tutor's US launch, after the owner amended D1; and five (OI-23 to OI-27) by the self-tutor. We are proceeding ahead of OI-3 under D34 and of OI-23 to OI-27 under D59, and each row says so.
