A student learns better when they choose who is teaching them, not just what.

That is the whole bet. Everything in this vault either supports it, prices it, or says honestly that we do not yet know. AI.tutor is a GCSE and A-level tutor built in Bengaluru for the UK market, where the student picks a named educator whose style they already recognise and the tutor teaches that way.

UnprovenNo pilot, no users, no revenue as at Sep 2026

How to read this

Every claim in these documents carries a tag in the right margin. Green means it was checked against a named source on a named date. Amber means it is a reasoned assumption that has not been confirmed. Red means it is unresolved and something depends on it. If a page of argument has no tags, it has not been written properly yet. Document 14 and the decision ledger are the registers those tags point to, so they carry none themselves.
