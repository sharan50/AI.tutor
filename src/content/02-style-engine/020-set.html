The parameter set, version 1

Twelve dimensions, each an integer from 0 to 100. Decision The seven dimensions named in the brief are all present; five were added because the seven did not span the differences visible in real teaching, and one of the additions is flagged as low-weight for a reason given below.

Warmth, encouragement rate and register are deliberately excluded from the twelve. They are the easiest dimensions to implement, the easiest to perceive, and they fail admission test 2: they change how a session sounds without changing what the student is asked to do. A style engine built on them would demonstrate beautifully and teach identically, and the D14 return-rate instrumentation would then be measuring nothing. Register is carried in the style card as free text for naturalness, is not scored, and is not adjustable by the student.

What this makes harder: presets will feel less distinct on a first read than they would if tone varied, which raises the risk that T-STY-02 fails for a reason that is real but not the reason we care about. That risk is accepted and noted in where this design is weak.

Decision D1714 September 2026: no tone dimension in the weighted set
