Conformance testing

A style that cannot be verified is a claim, not a feature. Four tests. None has been run.

TestWhat it establishesMethodPass conditionStatus
T-STY-01The tutor actually teaches at the point in the space it was told to.Run a fixed battery of objectives under a preset. Extract all twelve signatures from the transcripts automatically. Compare against the preset vector.At least ten of twelve dimensions within the tolerance band, and no dimension outside twice the band.Specified, not run
T-STY-02The difference between presets is perceptible to a human who was not told what to look for.Blind discrimination. A grader sees two transcripts of the same objective produced under two presets, plus the two style cards, and assigns which is which. Pre-registered sample size and binomial test.Assignment accuracy significantly above chance at a pre-registered level.Specified, not run
T-STY-03Style changes the teaching and never the truth.Same objective under all shipped presets. Compare cited item-bank nodes and correctness of every asserted step.100% overlap on the objective's required nodes, and no difference in correctness rate beyond sampling noise.Specified, not run
T-STY-04No licensed exemplar is reproduced in output. Applies only under D6; not applicable at launch.Longest-common-subsequence scan of every output against the licensed corpus.No output contains a contiguous match above the threshold set in the licence.Not applicable until D6 is triggered

T-STY-03 is the one that protects the business. The failure it guards against is a tutor that, asked to be brisk and exam-facing, quietly starts cutting corners on correctness, or that under a concrete-first preset avoids the formal statement altogether and leaves the student unable to answer the examined question. Style is a presentation-layer variation over a fixed content plane, and T-STY-03 is what turns that sentence from an intention into a property.

T-STY-02 is the one that tests the venture. If a blind grader cannot tell two presets apart, then D4's wedge has no substance to sell, whatever students say in a survey. It is deliberately scheduled before any creator is signed, at M3 in 11 Roadmap, so that a failure costs an engineering cycle rather than a contract.
