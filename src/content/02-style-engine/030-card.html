From object to style card

The model never sees the object. It sees a style card, a block of text generated from the object by a deterministic, versioned renderer.

Determinism matters for three reasons that are not obvious: a card can be regenerated exactly from a stored object, so audit is possible; a card can be diffed when a preset changes; and when a licence ends, deleting the object removes every card that could be produced from it, because the renderer is the only path by which one comes into existence. That is a statement about production, not about retention: a rendered card and a fitted vector are also held in Session.preset_snapshot, which is why 06 deletes the snapshot on the same line.

render(object, renderer_version) -> style_card_text

The renderer maps each dimension to a band and emits the instruction for that band. Bands, not raw numbers, because a model follows "do not state the general rule until the student has worked at least two instances" far more reliably than it follows "formalism_latency: 88".

Teaching style for this session.

Open with a specific instance, never with the general rule. Use numbers or a situation the student can check for themselves in one step.

Do not state the general rule until the student has worked at least two instances and has attempted to state it themselves. If they state it imperfectly, accept the substance and restate it precisely once.

Carry one example through the whole session. Change the numbers in it rather than introducing unrelated new contexts.

When the student is wrong, do not correct them. Ask a question whose answer will show them the error, and let them say what went wrong. Correct directly only if the same error survives two attempts.

Stay concrete. Generalise only after several instances, and signal when you are doing it.

Use a figure where it carries the idea better than a sentence; roughly two thirds of explanatory moves should have one.

Ask more than you assert. The student should be producing more text than you are.

Use plain language before notation. Introduce symbols only once the idea they name is already understood.

Do not warn about common wrong answers in advance. Let the student meet them.

Show about half the steps in a worked solution and leave the rest to the student.

Register: plain, unhurried, second person.

Note what the card is not. It contains no creator's name, no quotation, no phrasing attributable to anyone, and nothing that could not have been written by a teacher trainer describing a lesson. That is a deliberate property and it is the one that makes 04 tractable.
