Style in the learner's own likeness

The likeness carries no style. A self-tutor teaches from the same style object as any other tutor, and the learner's face and voice change only how its turns are presented. Style is still twelve numbers counted in a transcript, and the transcript is the same whether or not a self-tutor presents it (01).

None of the twelve dimensions reaches speech. Each is defined by what can be counted in a transcript, and the voice speaks that transcript verbatim (D64). Delivery is the same under every preset: the learner's own voice at one neutral delivery, with speaking rate, pitch and intonation set by the voice rendering, never by the preset, and the free-text register note does not reach the voice either. Tone was kept out of the weighted set on purpose (D17), and letting a preset vary delivery would bring it back through the voice. And style is defined, measured and tested on transcripts (T-STY-01, T-STY-02), so a style carried in the voice would be one that no test checks.

Everything else on this page applies unchanged. A learner's adjustments work as in how a student moves an axis, under the same clamps, and stay private to them. visual_density still governs figures, which render on screen beside the likeness. The style object gains no field for the likeness: the likeness is stored and deleted separately (06), so deleting a self-tutor leaves the learner's style untouched, and moving an axis never changes the likeness.

Named presets, by the creator's opt-in

Every archetype is available in the self-tutor. A named educator's preset is available in it only where that creator has opted in by a new term in the licence (D66): D5 licenses a name and a likeness to label a style, not that style taught in another person's face and voice. A creator who has not opted in is not offered in the self-tutor's chooser. Withdrawing the opt-in, or the end of the licence, removes the preset from every self-tutor on the kill switch's lines: new sessions lose it within fifteen minutes, and a session in progress falls back to the nearest archetype at its next turn. The label says whose style it is and that the creator does not appear (01). What the term must say is OI-27; the creator's continuing review right and the 14-day objection mechanism above apply unchanged.

The self-tutor in D14's experiment

D14's experiment compares named presets with unnamed archetypes on return rate. An uncontrolled self-tutor would confound it: a learner who comes back might be coming back for the likeness, not the style. So the self-tutor is a second factor, not a third arm (D68). Cohorts are given named presets or archetypes only, as D33 randomises availability, and separately the self-tutor on offer or not. The return-rate comparison reads the first factor and T-SLF-01 the second, with their interaction pre-registered: D66 offers only opted-in creators' presets inside the self-tutor, so the named-preset factor is weaker where the self-tutor is on. The sample this needs is Unknown until the experiment is designed (11).
