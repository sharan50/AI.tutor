Foundation vision

Foundation's expanded scope: the same instrument, a camera that sees the hands and the work and never the face, for other skills practised at a fixed station, such as cooking and the piano or keyboard, in schools for pupils of 4 to 18 and at home for families. The first version, for Reception to Year 2, does not change, and none of this is built before FM4. Which skills the home version covers is the owner's to scope.
