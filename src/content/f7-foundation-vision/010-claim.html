Foundation vision, in four sentences

Foundation vision is Foundation's expanded scope: once the first version, for Reception to Year 2, has passed its pilot, the same instrument would judge other skills practised at a fixed station, such as cooking at a counter and playing the piano or keyboard, for pupils of 4 to 18 in schools and, in parallel, in a home version for families (D130, D133). In schools, Foundation runs on the school's own servers and the teacher sees the class view, as now (D135); at home, a home unit connects to Foundation's cloud service, and the feedback comes through an app (D131). The camera is the only sensor: there is no microphone and no data from the instrument, so Foundation vision judges only what can be seen (D132). In schools, the pupil is shown nothing at any age, and the teacher acts (D93); at home, the app shows its feedback to adults, and to a learner of 13 or over who holds it, and a child under 13 is shown nothing (D134).

It could be that we expand after a pilot and proof of concept, expand to all the use cases where there is a certain amount of fixed positioning. So like evaluating cooking skills, evaluating piano or other musical instruments. And we can talk about the expanded scope as like Foundation vision or something along those lines.

DecisionD130, the owner's, 5 November 2026

The first version does not change. It goes to market as the classroom instrument for Reception to Year 2 that F1 to F6 describe. Foundation vision is not a fourth idea, and skills practised on the move are outside it, because no fixed camera can follow them.
