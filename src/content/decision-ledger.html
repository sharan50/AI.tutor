Decisions taken against advice, recorded as such

D4 and D5 together. The recommendation was to substitute institutional teachers for named creators, on the grounds that it converts the largest legal exposure into a distribution asset. The owner overruled it, reasoning that a named creator gives a student a tried and tested handle on how they want to be taught. That reasoning was accepted and became the design principle in document 02: the name is the handle, the style is the substance.

The risk ranking. The owner ranked child harm and data breach fourth of four. Recorded as given in document 12, with the engineering exception stated alongside it.
