Where the demonstration differs

The MVP demonstration was built to an earlier design and does not change. It answers a question and then asks the tester what they think; almost everything else the films show, it shows differently or not at all, and nothing it does is evidence for the Primer. Both generate their videos live; only the product checks them.

The Primer is the Primer the owner's three films show. Its pages describe it, and where a decision here departs from a film, the page says so and why. The MVP demonstration (D70 to D79) is unchanged, and the pages say where it differs.

Three of these matter beyond the look. The football thread, the films' central conversation, is the one conversation the demonstration stops. Its live clips differ from the product's videos in what guards them: the demonstration's pass no checks and say they are not checked, while the product's pass automatic checks before they play and say they are made by AI (D122, D126; P5). D122 supersedes D73's product half, that a video is never generated live for a child. And its speech path, built for adults, is no precedent for a child's audio, which P4 governs (P4). The guide's line that a child's voice waits on OI-17 was written for the demonstration under D72; counsel has since cleared a child speaking to the Primer, in the UK and the US, as the owner reported (D118).
