Primer: product

One child, a tablet of their own, and one question at a time. The child asks aloud, as most are expected to, or types; the Primer speaks a short checked answer at once while explainer videos are generated, sets out three positions at their strongest, follows the child's real question with evidence, reads back where the child has got to, and asks "Leave it there, or keep going?". This document shows one question explored end to end as the film on the front page shows it, then a question in a subject the parent has switched off and one about the child's own life: the moments where a product like this is judged.
