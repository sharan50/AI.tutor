What is promised, and the test behind each promise

Fifteen promises, each one a claim that could be false and each with a test; T-PRM-06 tests two of them. None has been run, because nothing has been built.

The status column is the same for every row and will stay that way until PM4 in P6. T-PRM-09 to T-PRM-15 are defined in P5; what a generated video may show is in P4.

The promise a parent most wants, that the Primer helps their child think more clearly, is not made. It is the claim the whole idea exists for, and nothing yet supports it; nor do the films, which are illustrative and made with AI. T-PRM-07, the reasoning test in P3, is the test that could earn it.
