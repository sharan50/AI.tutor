When the subject is switched off

A question in a module the parent has switched off gets a kind decline and nothing more: no lecture, no inquiry, no view, and no "Keep going".

A ten-year-old asks a question that falls in "Right and wrong", a sensitive module their parent has not switched on.

The Primer · module: Right and wrong, switched off · child aged ten · illustrative
Child

is it wrong to eat animals

Primer

That is a big question, and people disagree about it, including people who have thought about it for a long time. It is in a part of the Primer that is switched off at the moment, so it is not explored here. It is one to talk about with a grown-up you trust.

Button: Leave it there

Module "Right and wrong": sensitive, written for 10 and over, off until a parent switches it on (D46, D57); switched on, it would be explored only from reviewed packs, never from live generation (D127). The module switch (T-PRM-05): nothing from the module's packs is in the permitted set, and topic classification sorts the question into the switched-off module, so nothing is generated for it either. That classification is a weaker hold than the filter on packs. The close: "Keep going" never follows a decline (D115), so "Leave it there" stands alone, and the screen returns to "What's your question?" with no invitation to ask anything else. A decline is not a safety reply, so it may be spoken (D124); a safety reply, a triage stop and the screen shown without a connection are shown and never spoken (D115; P5). The parent's record shows the category, "a question in Right and wrong, switched off", never the child's words (D48). The child was told before their first session what a parent can see, and nothing about the record is said mid-conversation; exactly what a child must be told is Open OI-14.
