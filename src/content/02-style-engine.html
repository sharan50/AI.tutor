Style engine

The riskiest component in the build. Get it wrong in one direction and the personas are catchphrases and a voice filter. Get it wrong in the other and every creator relationship becomes a content licence negotiation.

What a teaching style actually is

Style is not tone. Tone is the part that is easy to imitate and contributes least to whether a student understands. The part that teaches is structural: where an explanation enters from, what is withheld and for how long, what happens when the student is wrong.

So style is represented as a structured object over dimensions that are observable in a recorded lesson and owned by nobody. A written style card is generated from that object and given to the model, because models follow prose more naturally than they follow a schema.

The dimensions

First cut. Each has a defined range and an observable signature, meaning a thing you could point at in a transcript to score it.

DimensionRangeObservable signature
Point of entryConcrete situation → formal statementWhat appears in the first 60 seconds: an object and a question, or a definition
Lateness of formalismImmediate → withheld until derivedPosition in the session at which notation first appears
Example strategyOne running example → many variedCount of distinct scenarios per concept
Error responseDirect correction → predict then falsifyWhether a wrong answer is corrected or used to set up a prediction that fails visibly
Digression toleranceStrict → discursiveProportion of turns not on the target objective
Visual densityVerbal → visual-firstDiagrams per concept; whether the diagram leads or illustrates
Pace of abstractionSlow generalisation → fastNumber of instances before the general case is stated

These seven are a starting proposal and should be treated as wrong until a working GCSE teacher has been through them. The most likely errors are that error response and pace of abstraction are correlated rather than independent, and that at least one dimension that matters to exam performance, probably something about how mark-scheme language is modelled, is missing entirely.

Presets, and the student's dial

A named educator is a preset: a point in the space, derived by watching their published teaching and scoring each dimension. Nothing of theirs is ingested, stored or reproduced. The artefact is a scored vector and a description written in our own words.

Selecting a preset sets all seven dimensions. The student may then move any of them individually. This is the point of the whole design: the name gives them a handle they can actually reach for, and the dials let them discover that what they liked was the slow abstraction rather than the person.

This is also the mechanism by which the venture learns something nobody else has. If enough students start from a preset and move a dimension in a consistent direction, that is evidence about how teaching should work, and it belongs to us rather than to the creator.

The archetype set, which is what proves zero-persona operation

Decision D11 says the product runs with no signed creators. That is only true if a full set of usable styles exists before any creator is approached, so here it is. Each archetype is a named point in the seven-dimension space, written by us, owned by us, and shippable on day one.

ArchetypePositionWho it is for
Ground UpConcrete entry, formalism withheld, predict-then-falsify, one running example, visual-first, slow abstractionA student whose mental model is wrong and does not know it
Straight LineFormal entry, formalism immediate, direct correction, many varied examples, verbal, fast abstractionA student whose model is sound and whose recall is slow. The April student
Worked ThroughFormal entry, formalism immediate, direct correction, one running example, visual, slow abstractionA student who can follow a method but cannot start one
Long Way RoundConcrete entry, formalism late, predict-then-falsify, many varied, visual-first, slow abstraction, high digression toleranceA student who is interested and not under time pressure
Exam RoomFormal entry, immediate, direct correction, many varied, verbal, fast abstraction, zero digressionA student with three weeks left

A named preset is therefore never a new capability. It is a label on a point in a space that already covers the useful ground, plus a scored vector that may sit between two archetypes. This is what makes the kill switch cheap: removing a creator reverts the preset to its nearest archetype, and the student loses a name rather than a way of being taught.

Proof of D11Five archetypes, no creator required

Honest limit on that proof. The archetypes make the product operable without creators; they do not make it marketable without them. Document 07 records that creator acquisition remains the single point of failure for Route A distribution.

Conformance, made concrete

Verification, because an unverifiable style is a claim

The two hand-written transcripts in document 01 are the reference behaviour. The harness scores a generated session on the same observables and compares it with the style object that was in force.

ObservableHow it is scoredFailure
Turn index of first notationCount of tutor turns before symbols appearNotation in turn 1 under a withheld-formalism style
Wrong-answer handlingWhether the next turn corrects or elicits a predictionDirect correction under a predict-then-falsify style
Distinct scenarios per objectiveCount of separate contexts introducedFour scenarios under a one-running-example style
Off-objective turnsProportion not advancing the stated objectiveAny, under Exam Room

Conformance rate is reported, not aspired to. It is also the evidence a creator will want before signing, and the evidence a regulator would want if anyone ever asked what the system was doing.

What is deliberately not being built

No LoRA adapters, no fine-tuned weights carrying a creator's style. Weights cannot be cleanly revoked; you cannot un-train them, and a kill switch that requires retraining and redeployment is not a kill switch. Retrieval and prompting can be withdrawn in the time it takes to update a record.

What this costs: the fidelity ceiling is lower than fine-tuning would give. If weight-level style is ever genuinely needed, that is a new decision with new legal advice attached, not a configuration change.

The fallback boundary

Launch personas pass a student usefulness test. The contingency, if market feedback says they are recognisably not the person, is exemplar conditioning on licensed creator material. That path has to be designed now or it becomes a rewrite later, so the boundary is defined here.

The interface

The style object gains one optional field: a set of exemplar references, scoped to a single creator, held separately from the item bank, and independently revocable. Nothing else changes. The style card generator reads the field if present and includes exemplars in the conditioning context; the conformance harness scores the same seven dimensions either way.

What changes when it is switched on

  • The creator agreement becomes a content licence rather than a name and likeness licence, which is a negotiation rather than a signature.
  • Termination becomes an operation: exemplars must be purgeable per creator, with the purge provable.
  • Exam board material can never enter this path, since the exemplars are the creator's own teaching and nothing else.
  • The system starts generating explanations in a creator's manner on topics they never covered, which is the specific thing that makes educators hesitate. The agreement needs a review right for that.

What does not change

Zero-persona operation, the dimension set, the conformance harness, the student's dials, and the kill-switch path. That is the point of putting style in an object rather than in a prompt or in weights.

The trigger, which is narrower than "it underperformed"

Two failures will feel identical and have opposite fixes. Named presets ship alongside unnamed archetypes from day one, and the instrument is return rate rather than satisfaction score.

ObservationDiagnosisAction
Named presets do not beat unnamed archetypes on repeat sessionsThe wedge is weakDo not licence material. Higher fidelity cannot rescue a handle nobody wants
Named presets beat archetypes, but feedback says it does not sound like himFidelity gapTrigger the exemplar path for that creator

Proposed threshold: exemplar conditioning goes live only when named presets show a clear retention advantage and qualitative feedback attributes the shortfall to fidelity rather than relevance. The number behind "clear" is not set, because it depends on a cohort size we cannot estimate yet.

Voice

Voice cloning is off the launch path entirely and becomes a separate opt-in limb of the creator licence. It is the highest-risk signal on both sides of the design, and it is the specific thing Ofcom named when it warned service providers about users building virtual clones of real people.
