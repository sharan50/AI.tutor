Primer: thesis

The Primer is a tutor for a child's why-questions, for ages 8 to 13, on a tablet of its own. Its case begins with time, in the owner's view: every child asks why, and most evenings nobody has the time to answer properly Assumed. A child asks aloud or by typing; the Primer answers aloud while a drawn explainer builds on the screen, starts from facts a person has checked, and asks until the child can say where they stand, and why. On the owner's reading it is the idea an AI can do best, because conversation is what current models do well, though every fact and drawing it shows must first be written and checked by people. It is also the idea whose buyer, reputation and proof are least established, and this document says so before anything else.

P2 Primer: product

The Primer Ages 8 to 13

A tutor for a child's why-questions, designed to ask what they think rather than tell them.

"Every child asks why. Most evenings, nobody has the time to answer properly." That is the owner's view, untested Assumed, and the Primer's case begins there. It is a tablet of its own, one per child, with nothing else on it. A child asks aloud or by typing; the Primer answers aloud as a drawn explainer builds on the screen: a checked fact with its source, three positions from named traditions at their strongest, then questions until the child can say where they stand, and why. It gives no view of its own where people disagree, and on a choice in a child's own future it neither encourages nor discourages, setting out only the reality and what the schools of thought say. Parents choose which topics are switched on.

Who pays is the open question: parents, with no exam to prepare for, buying a tablet as well as the service.

Designed, not built. Nothing here has been tried with a child yet.
