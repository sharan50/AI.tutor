What Y Combinator asked for, and where the Primer parts from it

Y Combinator's Fall 2026 requests for startups include one called "The Primer". It opens by observing that the best education has always been one-to-one tutoring, that Aristotle taught Alexander, and that the privilege was reserved for the few. It names the Primer of The Diamond Age as the long-term goal, and asks for adaptive teaching of reading, writing and arithmetic to young children, close to the quality of a private tutor, at consumer scale, supplementing teachers rather than replacing them, and starting as something parents buy for basic skills. Secondary write-ups give the ages as 4 to 10. The page itself could not be opened from the build environment; this is a paraphrase from search summaries of it.

AssumedCV-01, awaiting the owner's check

Where the Primer agrees, taking the request as CV-01 records it Assumed. One-to-one tutoring is the standard to aim at. The tutor supplements teachers rather than replacing them. Parents are the first buyers. And the long-term goal the request names, a tutor that teaches a child not only to read but to think and reason, is the Primer's goal exactly.

Where it parts, and why. As CV-01 records it, the request starts with reading, writing and arithmetic for young children, bought by parents for basic skills, and names the thinking Primer as the long-term goal Assumed (CV-01). The owner's order differs (D94, which keeps D42's). Separately from the order, in the owner's view a five-year-old does not sit still to be taught by a screen, reading and writing are taught physically by a person guiding a finger along print and forming letters with the child, and at that age managing the child is half of teaching them Assumed (OA-28). The thinking tutor for older children is, on that view, the part an AI can deliver now, though the tablet it would run on still has to be designed and made (D105); so the Primer starts with what the request names as its long-term goal.

Foundation does not go first. Rejected: the order of YC's request as CV-01 records it Assumed, which starts with basic skills bought by parents and names the thinking Primer as the long-term goal.

DecisionD42, 22 October 2026

This is a real risk, not a debating point: an application that argues with the request's sequence must do so openly, or it reads as not having read the request. Since 29 October 2026 the order rests on D94, the owner's order given without reasons, and D42's reasons are withdrawn with Foundation's design for a parent at home; which idea goes first says so.
