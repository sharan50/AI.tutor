Price.Untested. OA-10 decides the anchor and is resolvable at M1. Inventing a price would put a number into an investor conversation with nothing behind it.

Prices for the Primer and Foundation.No anchor exists. OA-22, resolvable by asking parents, and OA-51, by asking schools.
What the Tutor teaches in the US, and with it the specifications, examiners and grade range it would be built on there, and how it would reach US schools.D1, as amended, opens the US alongside the UK where resources allow, and everything in 03 and 08 is written for the UK. Whether Foundation is built at all.OA-51 and OA-52 are answerable by asking schools, staff and parents; FM2 tests the reader and FM3 the alerts; OI-28 is counsel's. F6.
