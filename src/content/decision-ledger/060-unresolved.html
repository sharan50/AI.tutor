Decisions deliberately not taken

Recorded here so that their absence is visible rather than looking like an oversight.

Not decidedWhy not
Price.Untested. OA-10 decides the anchor and is resolvable at M1. Inventing a price would put a number into an investor conversation with nothing behind it.
Creator fee, and whether it is a flat fee or a revenue share.Commercial, and both structures are drafted in the heads of terms. The number is not a design decision.
Model vendor.The selection procedure is specified in 06 and runs at M2 against a frozen battery. Choosing now would be choosing on memory rather than measurement. The Primer's model and its speech vendors, which turn a child's speech into text and speak the answers, are not chosen either, for the same reason, and D24 is the gate each must pass (P5, speech).
Which entity contracts with the parent.OI-7. A counsel question with tax, jurisdiction and transfer consequences.
Hiring and funding plans.Both depend on the content cost from M2 and the payback ratio from M5. Neither exists.
Whether to exercise D6 or D9.D6 has a decision rule and a trigger (11). D9 remains off the launch path and no trigger has been written for it, which is itself the decision.
Which idea goes first, the Tutor or the Primer.Foundation does not go first, by the owner's order (D94). Between the other two, the field research decides, against the tests on which idea goes first.
Whether the three ideas are one product with three versions, or three products.It turns on whether a child would move from one to the next, which nothing in the vault can yet say. Since 31 October 2026 the Primer is a tablet of its own (D105), so one product could share an account, a brand and a back end (D40) but not a device; that bears on the answer without giving it. One product or three.
Prices for the Primer's tablet and its service, and for Foundation.No anchor exists. For the Primer, it is not decided either whether the tablet is sold, lent or bundled with the subscription; OA-22 and OA-54 are resolvable by asking parents and with a landing page, PM5 tests real purchases at a real price, and what the tablet costs to make, ship, support and certify is Unknown (RP7). For Foundation, OA-51, by asking schools.
What the Tutor teaches in the US, and with it the specifications, examiners and grade range it would be built on there, and how it would reach US schools.D1, as amended, opens the US alongside the UK where resources allow, and everything in 03 and 08 is written for the UK. The economics instrument in econ/ models a US examination like Advanced Placement, a simplification its own LIMITS.md records, not a choice. The owner decides; how much UK content carries across is item C5 in the instrument's open items.
What "where resources allow" means, for D1 and D51.No test is written for it. The economics instrument opens the Tutor's US market nine months after the UK, which matches the fallback; a launch of both together has not been modelled, and modelling it is the first way to price the question.
Whether the self-tutor is priced separately.Nothing anchors a price for it, and whether parents will consent to it at all (OA-40) comes first.
Where Foundation runs: on the school's own servers or in the cloud.D90 allows both. FM2 measures what each costs, and a school's due diligence may decide it (OA-14). F5.
Whether Foundation is built at all.OA-51 and OA-52 are answerable by asking schools, staff and parents; FM2 tests the reader and FM3 the alerts; OI-28 is counsel's. F6.
Who designs and makes the Primer's tablet, and where.D105 decides what the tablet is, not who designs or makes it. PM4 runs the built Primer on a stand-in tablet locked to the Primer; the dedicated tablet is designed in parallel and made only after PM4's evidence, and what it must meet before sale is counsel's question (OI-31). P6.
What the parent's screen runs on.The sleep film shows what the parent's screen holds (topics, questions, the child's recorded view and the module switches), not what it runs on. P4 and P5 put it on the parent's own phone or computer, behind the verified adult's sign-in and never on the child's tablet, which has no browser and no other apps (D105); no decision records that, so it is the owner's to confirm. Wherever it runs, it must keep a flagged exchange from the parent (D107) and the settings from the child (D117). P5.
