D39: the front door and the owner views

Dated 17 September 2026. A decision about how the vault is read, recorded here because it sets who a change is discussed with and what a generated page may contain.

D39. The vault has a generated front door and one generated page per owner; ownership is set at section level in roles.json; and a fact reaches a view only by transcluding a block marked in the fragment that states it.
Driven by: a person joining should read what they own and nothing else, and until now a newcomer landed on a contents page listing every document, every decision and every open item. The front door is a short summary generated from the ledger, the open-items register, the dependency map and the marked key blocks; each owner's page names their documents and sections, their decisions and open items, what binds them, and a reading path with cumulative word counts. Nothing on a generated page is written twice: the only hand-written prose is one capped frame per page, and every count is computed at build time. Rejected: hand-written role pages, because they drift; page-level ownership, because the two route documents would each be split between three owners; and including whole fragments in role pages, which would make each page a copy of the vault. Makes harder: a prose edit on a generated page is lost on the next build, so a frame lives in roles.json and everything else in the fragments; and ownership changes only through roles.json, never by editing a page.
