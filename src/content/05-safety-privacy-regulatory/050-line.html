The line that goes on the marketing site

We keep what your child types to the tutor and what the tutor says back, which topics they got right and wrong, the progress estimate we show you, the account and billing details you gave us, and a record that we checked you are an adult. If your child tells the tutor something that worries us, we keep a record of that too, and a trained person reads it.

We do not sell any of it. We do not use it to train anyone's AI model. We do not use it to advertise to your child. We do not work anything out about your child beyond how they are doing in the subject they are studying.

Our team is in India and in the UK, and your child's data is handled in both.

You can delete it whenever you want. It goes from the product straight away, from our systems within seven days and from our backups within thirty-five. Two things we cannot delete: billing records, which we are required to keep for six years, and any safeguarding record, because deleting one could put a child at risk.

DecisionThis is the line. It is meant to be used unedited.

Every clause in that paragraph is load-bearing and traceable to a commitment in this repository. That is the test of whether it can be published.

ClauseWhat makes it true
The list of what is keptEvery row of the data table above, including the tutor's own replies, the progress estimate, the age check record and safeguarding records. An earlier version of this line named three of eight rows and called itself complete; that was the single most misleading sentence in this vault and it is why the line is now four sentences rather than one.
"handled in India and in the UK"D1: the build base is Bengaluru and the market is the UK. A parent asks where their child's data goes before they ask anything else, and the transfer mechanism is still an unresolved gap below. Saying it plainly is the minimum; it is not a substitute for resolving it.
"Two things we cannot delete"Billing retention (OA-13) and safeguarding records. Both are real exceptions to a deletion promise and both now sit in the same breath as the promise rather than in a table further down.
"We do not sell it"No data sale, no data sharing for consideration, no third-party analytics SDK in the learner surface.
"not to train anyone's AI model"Decision D24: zero-retention, no-training vendor terms as a procurement gate. This clause is false without that contract, which is why it is a gate and not a preference.
"not to advertise to your child"No advertising in the product and no ad-tech in the stack.
"nothing beyond how they are doing in the subject"The not-inferred list above, plus D25. The progress estimate sits inside this boundary because it is computed from performance on validated items in the enrolled subject and from nothing else; that is exactly why it is named in the first sentence rather than relied on to be covered by silence.
"seven days, thirty-five days"The deletion SLA. The backup figure is stated rather than omitted, because that is the clause an auditor checks.
