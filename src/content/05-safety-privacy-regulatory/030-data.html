Staff access to transcripts

Transcripts are not browsable by anyone. Access requires one of exactly three conditions, is role-limited, and every access is logged with a reason:

  1. A safety escalation has fired on that session.
  2. That turn has been drawn in the T-GND-01 accuracy sample and requires human adjudication.
  3. The account holder has raised a support request about that session.

The model vendor

No model vendor is used unless the contract provides zero retention of prompts and outputs, and no use of our data for training. This is not a preference. The marketing line below says "we do not use it to train anyone's AI model", and that clause is only true if the vendor contract makes it true. A vendor that cannot offer it is disqualified regardless of model quality, and the term is checked at procurement and at every renewal.

Deletion

On request: inaccessible in the product immediately, purged from primary stores within 7 days, aged out of backups within 35 days. The backup figure is stated on the marketing site rather than hidden, because a deletion promise with a silent exception is the kind of thing an ICO audit finds.
