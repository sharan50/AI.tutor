The module registry and the switch

The registry is the single source the composer reads. For each module it holds the identifier and version, the age band, the sensitive flag, the default switch, the pack and bank entries it contains, its review status and sign-off, and a live flag the company controls. For each child it holds the parent's switch for every module offered in the child's band, whether speech is on, and the account's market.

For each entry it also holds what D113's rules need: the market its evidence is for, the UK, the US or both; each figure it states, with its source, the group it counts, whether that source is an estimate, and a date to check it again, after which the composer does not permit it (P3); every named living person it describes; and its explainer specification, with that specification's review and live flag (above). Claim support reads the figure fields, and a pull by person writes to the person field (below).

The composer filters before it matches: an entry is permitted only if its module is live, switched on for this child, and written for the child's band. Nothing from an excluded module can enter the permitted set, and the verifier's scope check re-checks every cited identifier against the filter, so a fault in one layer is caught by the other. That is T-PRM-05, deterministic and blocking on every release.

DecisionD46, 22 October 2026

The filter also holds evidence to the child's market, since D113 gives each child the figures and cases of their own market, the UK's or the US's; a child whose market has none gets "not yet", never another country's figure. T-PRM-05 checks the market with the rest.

The topic of a child's own future carries two settings D106 fixes, wherever P3 places it among the modules (P3): it is offered in every band from 8 to 13, where D57 offers sensitive modules only from 10, and it starts switched on, where D46 starts sensitive modules off. A parent can turn it off.

Switch state is read at every turn, as the Tutor reads its persona flag, so a parent's change applies from the child's next turn. The band, the switches and the market are settings the verified adult makes, not memory of the child (D117). The child's band comes from the age in years the verified adult gave when creating the profile, never a date of birth, and the child cannot change it. A parent who gives an older age can open modules written for older children; that rests on the adult's word and is a limit, recorded in P4 (D57).
