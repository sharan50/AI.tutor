The module registry and the switch

The registry is the single source the composer reads for what a turn may use. For each module it holds the identifier and version, the age band, the sensitive flag, the default switch, the pack and bank entries it contains, its review status and sign-off, and a live flag the company controls. For each source on the reviewed allow-list it holds its review, the age ranges it is reviewed for, the market its evidence is for and a live flag; and it holds a live flag for each prompt pattern and video style, and one for live generation itself (below). For each child it holds the parent's switch for every module offered in the child's band, whether speech is on, and the account's market.

For each entry it also holds what D113's rules need: the market its evidence is for, the UK, the US or both; each figure it states, with its source, the group it counts, whether that source is an estimate, and a date to check it again, after which the composer does not permit it (P3); and every named living person it describes, whose facts and stories come only from reviewed entries (D127). Claim support reads the figure fields, and a pull by person writes to the person field (below).

The composer filters before it matches: an entry is permitted only if its module is live, switched on for this child, and written for the child's band. Nothing from an excluded module can enter the permitted set, and the verifier's scope check re-checks every cited identifier against the filter, so a fault in one layer is caught by the other. That is T-PRM-05, deterministic and blocking on every release.

DecisionD46, 22 October 2026

Generated content meets the switch through its topic. An answer from the allow-list cites no module's entry, so the filter above cannot hold it on its own. Triage classifies each question's topic against the modules, and the composer classifies each explainer's concept the same way, before a script is written or the video cache is read, so the child's switches and D127 hold a cached video as they hold a new one (above): a topic whose module is switched off, or outside the child's band, gets the decline triage gives, and nothing is retrieved for it. That is a weaker hold than the entry filter, because a classifier can misplace a question, and T-PRM-05 tests it with a battery of questions on every topic switched off (above). The same classification finds the questions D127 holds to reviewed content: facts and stories about named living people, and every sensitive module, come only from reviewed entries and packs, never from live generation. The scope check tells those questions apart again before anything is spoken.

The filter also holds evidence to the child's market, on the allow-list as in the entries, since D113 gives each child the figures and cases of their own market, the UK's or the US's; a child whose market has none gets "not yet", never another country's figure. T-PRM-05 checks the market with the rest. Each allow-listed source is reviewed for the ranges it suits, the registry records them, and the composer retrieves only sources reviewed for the child's range of the Children's code, 8 and 9, 10 to 12, or 13; the suitability check then holds every generated sentence and script to that range (above).

The topic of a child's own future carries two settings D106 fixes, wherever P3 places it among the modules (P3): it is offered in every band from 8 to 13, where D57 offers sensitive modules only from 10, and it starts switched on, where D46 starts sensitive modules off. A parent can turn it off.

Switch state is read at every turn, as the Tutor reads its persona flag, so a parent's change applies from the child's next turn. The band, the switches and the market are settings the verified adult makes, not memory of the child (D117). The child's band, and their range within it, come from the age in years the verified adult gave when creating the profile, never a date of birth, and the child cannot change either. A parent who gives an older age can open modules written for older children; that rests on the adult's word and is a limit, recorded in P4 (D57).
