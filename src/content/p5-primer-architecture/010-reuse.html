What it reuses from the Tutor

Most of the Primer's service exists once the Tutor's platform does; its tablet, its speech, its live videos and its selection model do not. The service's pieces are the ones specified in 06, re-aimed at questions instead of objectives and at the Primer the owner's films show (D101), as the owner's decisions of 4 November 2026 change it (D118 to D129). The films are illustrative and made with AI; this page is the design they describe. None of 06's five planes holds a device, so the tablet is a sixth.

ComponentIn the TutorIn the Primer
Account, age assurance, billingA verified adult holds the account (05).The same. The child is a profile under the parent's account, and each tablet is paired to one child (below).
DeviceNone; no plane in 06 holds one.The Primer's own tablet, one per child, locked to the Primer (D105, D116).
SpeechOut only, for the self-tutor, in the learner's own voice (06).In and out, and the main way in (D123). Counsel has cleared a child speaking to the Primer (D118), and the verified adult agrees to it at setup (D129); the child's speech becomes text and the audio is thrown away (D102). The Primer answers in a stock voice (below).
ComposerSelects item-bank nodes for one objective, deterministically (06).Holds the stage of the answer's one shape (D110) and retrieves each stage's sources before generation: a reviewed entry or pack first, where one exists, and otherwise passages from the reviewed allow-list (D125). A topic switched off gets nothing. It retrieves again when the question shifts.
GeneratorA pinned, bought model (D22).The same, given a wording card in place of a style card, and never the child's name or age. It writes the instant answer and each later stage from the retrieved sources, a script for each explainer, and the read-back from the child's own words.
VerifierFour blocking checks, on a different pinned model (D27; 06).Citation presence, claim support and scope, re-aimed at the retrieved sources, plus attribution, the contested question, manner, no direction and suitability: eight checks, run on every turn and every script (below). The mathematics check is not used: every figure the Primer states must appear in its source, so generation does no arithmetic.
Figure rendererFigures drawn from a specification in the item, never generated (D28; 06).Not used. A figure is shown as text from its source, and explainers are generated videos (below).
VideoThe self-tutor's clips, rendered after the gate from passed text, labelled, streamed and never stored (06).A video model, after the checks, generates each explainer live from a visual brief with no words and no figures; its words, figures and voice-over come from its checked script. Its frames pass an image check, it is labelled as made by AI, and it may be cached by concept and reused (D122, D126; below).
Selection modelNone: the self-tutor speaks the text on screen (06).Trained for the purpose. It chooses for each checked sentence whether it is spoken, shown or both, and writes nothing (D124; below).
Gate and fallbackA validated item, verbatim.A sentence no source supports is cut. A blocked turn gets a reviewed entry or pack verbatim where one exists, otherwise a fixed question or "not yet". A failed video is not shown, and the spoken answer stands.
CloseNone.The read-back, then "Leave it there, or keep going?" (D103, D117).
LoggerThe turn record.Identifiers, verdicts and timings, a video only by its identifier, and the kept summary. No turn record holds audio or video, and a conversation's words are deleted at its close (D104; below).
Safety planeClassification, escalation routing and session caps (06).The same, with triage on every turn, a review at the close of each conversation (D107) and the time cap on "Keep going".
Kill switchA registry flag re-read at every turn (06).A live flag on every module, pack, entry, named person, allow-list source, prompt pattern and video style, and one on live generation itself, re-read at every turn and reaching every cached video (below).
Mastery modelObjective-level mastery.Not used. There is no mastery of philosophy, only summaries of a child's questions and positions (D104).
Style engineA parameter object, presets and a style card (02).A wording card: plain, brisk and pitched for the band (D112). No presets, and the Primer never speaks as a real person (D47).

The new engineering is the tablet; speech in and out; retrieval from the reviewed allow-list, more than once a conversation; live explainer videos, with their scripts and the image check; the selection model; triage with the own-future fence; the registry filter, and the topic classification that carries the switch to generated content; the composer that holds the answer's shape; five new checks; and the read-back. That is an argument for building the ideas on one platform (D40). It is not an argument for which goes first, which is set out in which idea goes first.

The demonstration differs. The MVP demonstration was built to an earlier design and does not change: it generates its clips live with no checks, labels them "not checked" and gives no sources, corrects an adult's speech before sending it, keeps nothing at all, and stops the football thread as a question about the asker's own life. Each is a difference from this page, listed in P2.
