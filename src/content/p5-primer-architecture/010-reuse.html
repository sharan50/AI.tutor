What it reuses from the Tutor

Most of the Primer's service exists once the Tutor's platform does; its tablet, its speech and its drawings do not. The service's pieces are the ones specified in 06, re-aimed at questions instead of objectives and at the Primer the owner's films show (D101). The films are illustrative and made with AI; this page is the design they describe. None of 06's five planes holds a device, so the tablet is a sixth.

ComponentIn the TutorIn the Primer
Account, age assurance, billingA verified adult holds the account (05).The same. The child is a profile under the parent's account, and each tablet is paired to one child (below).
DeviceNone; no plane in 06 holds one.The Primer's own tablet, one per child, locked to the Primer (D105, D116).
SpeechOut only, for the self-tutor, in the learner's own voice (06).In and out from launch: the child's speech becomes text and the audio is thrown away, ahead of OI-17 (D102); the Primer answers in a stock voice (below).
ComposerSelects item-bank nodes for one objective, deterministically (06).Holds the stage of the answer's one shape (D110) and selects each stage's bank and pack entries from enabled modules only; matches again when the question shifts.
GeneratorA pinned, bought model (D22).The same, given a wording card in place of a style card, and never the child's name or age. It places the entries' reviewed sentences and writes what the conversation composes around them.
VerifierFour blocking checks, on a different pinned model (D27; 06).Citation presence, claim support and scope, re-aimed, plus attribution, the contested question, manner and no direction: seven checks (below). The mathematics check is not used: every figure and ratio the Primer states is stored in its entry, so generation does no arithmetic.
Figure rendererFigures drawn from a specification in the item, never generated (D28; 06).Explainers drawn by code from a reviewed specification, in time with the voice (D111; below).
Gate and fallbackA validated item, verbatim.A reviewed entry, verbatim, with its drawing, or "not yet".
CloseNone.The read-back, then "Leave it there, or keep going?" (D103, D117).
LoggerThe turn record.Identifiers, verdicts and timings, and the kept summary. No turn record holds audio or video, and a conversation's words are deleted at its close (D104; below).
Safety planeClassification, escalation routing and session caps (06).The same, with triage on every turn, a review at the close of each conversation (D107) and the time cap on "Keep going".
Kill switchA registry flag re-read at every turn (06).A live flag on every module, pack, entry, explainer and named person, re-read at every turn, reaching what the tablet caches.
Mastery modelObjective-level mastery.Not used. There is no mastery of philosophy, only summaries of a child's questions and positions (D104).
Style engineA parameter object, presets and a style card (02).A wording card: plain, brisk and pitched for the band (D112). No presets, and the Primer never speaks as a real person (D47).

The new engineering is the tablet; speech in and out; the explainer renderer; triage with the own-future fence; the registry filter; matching a child's question to catalogue entries, more than once a conversation; the composer that holds the answer's shape; four new checks; and the read-back. That is an argument for building the ideas on one platform (D40). It is not an argument for which goes first, which is set out in which idea goes first.

The demonstration differs. The MVP demonstration was built to an earlier design and does not change: it generates its clips live, corrects an adult's speech before sending it, keeps nothing at all, and stops the football thread as a question about the asker's own life. Each is a difference from this page, listed in P2.
