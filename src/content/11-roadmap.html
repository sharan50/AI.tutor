The two things already settled

Milestones are evidence, not features. Each is stated as a thing that becomes known: whether a parent will pay, whether named presets beat unnamed archetypes, whether an examiner-validated item bank can be produced at a cost the price point supports.

Named presets and unnamed archetypes ship together on day one. The instrument is return rate rather than satisfaction score, because the two failure modes, a weak wedge and a fidelity gap, feel identical in a survey and have opposite fixes. The exemplar path in document 02 unlocks only on a clear retention advantage combined with feedback attributing the shortfall to fidelity.
