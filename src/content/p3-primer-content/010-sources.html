Where every statement and drawing comes from

Every factual statement the Primer makes cites a reviewed explanation, and every philosophical position it presents cites a reviewed inquiry pack that names the thinker or tradition. A question neither can answer gets an honest "not yet" and becomes a request for new content.

DecisionD44, 22 October 2026

This is the Tutor's founding rule, that nothing reaches a learner which cannot be traced to a checked source (03), carried into a domain where the risk is influence rather than a wrong mark. A Primer allowed to answer from the model's general knowledge would be a free chatbot with a child's interface, and nothing about it could be warranted to a parent. The films widen what the rule covers, not the rule: a drawing is content as much as a sentence is, and D113 adds how a figure is said, with D44 unchanged.

Nothing is composed for speech alone. The text sent to the voice equals the checked text on screen, and samples of what the voice says are transcribed and compared with it (T-PRM-11). Every row is also checked for manner: no "I", no child's name, no praise (D108, T-PRM-09). Both tests are defined in P5.

A child's words belong to the conversation, never to the content. Typed words appear as typed, in the child's own spelling (D109). Spoken words become text at once and the audio is thrown away (D102; P5), ahead of counsel's answer on whether a child's voice is biometric data (OI-17). Recognition errs more on children's speech than on adults', and more the younger the child, Assumed (CV-11); whether it is accurate enough is OA-55. The read-back at the close gives the child's position in plain words, as the Primer's sentence, which may repeat a short phrase of the child's where it states their position, never more, and the child can correct it before it is kept (D117; P4). The verifier holds a read-back to what the child said and blocks one that moves their view towards a position (P5); whether summaries are faithful enough is OA-56.

The cost is stated in D44 itself: at launch the Primer will say "not yet" often, and how often is Unknown until children use it (OA-32). That cost is accepted because the alternative is a product whose most important property, that it can be trusted with a child's questions, rests on nothing. The films raise it: an entry or pack goes live only with its reviewed explainer, so one still waiting for its drawing is "not yet".

The demonstration answers any question, labels everything "not checked" and gives no sources (D71); none of that is the product's (P2).
