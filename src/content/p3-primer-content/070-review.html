Who writes, and who reviews

People review in two places: before a child sees anything, for the reviewed core, the allow-list, the stories of named living people and every sensitive module; and after, for a sample of what is generated live.

People review a sample of the generated scripts and videos after the fact, and every one a child or a parent reports. This is assurance, not a gate before a child sees anything.

Decision D128taken while writing, 4 November 2026; the owner's to keep or reverse

Nothing reaches a child that has not passed the automatic checks, but most of it is never seen by a person first (D122). The checks have residual errors that no one would otherwise find, and the sample is how they are found: the Tutor's verifier sends every automated failure to a person (06), and D128 extends that to what passed.

The writer and the reviewer are never the same person, the rule the Tutor applies to its item bank (03). Writers are philosophy educators, including people trained in Philosophy for Children, primary and lower-secondary teachers, and subject specialists for the explanation bank; the films add statistics specialists for figures. Fact-checkers review every figure in an entry, every story of a named person and every source proposed for the allow-list.

Every reviewer also checks the manner rules, whatever the content: no "I", no child's name and no praise (D108).

The standing review group is the one page 13 requires: child-safety and education expertise, with the power to block a release, inspect red-team results and require a rollback (13). Because the owner wants the UK and the US at once (D51), review includes whether a module reads acceptably to families across the range of beliefs in both countries, and whether one catalogue can serve both is an assumption, OA-27. For figures and cases the bank and the allow-list are already two, since evidence follows the child's market (D113).

Provenance is recorded per entry and per source, every change is reviewed again, and a figure or a source is checked again by its date, because careers data and odds go stale while an entry stays the same. Live answers move this work rather than end it: the reviewed core, the stories of named living people and the sensitive modules' packs are still written and reviewed in advance, the allow-list must be reviewed and kept, and the sample is reviewed afterwards, in two markets. This is a rate-limited human process that does not get cheaper when the next model ships, which makes it the Primer's equivalent of the Tutor's examiner supply (12, R7). Unknown the cost of a module and the number of modules a review group can clear; PM2 measures both. The sample's size and the reviewers' time are also Unknown, set with the standing review group. RP5 is the risk that the reviewers of the sensitive modules, the allow-list and the sample become the bottleneck.
