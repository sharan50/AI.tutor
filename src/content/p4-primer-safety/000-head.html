Primer: safety, privacy and regulatory

The Primer's users are 8 to 13, its subject invites the questions children find hardest, it is a tablet that hears a child speak and shows them videos a model generates while they wait, and one launch market is the US, where AI chatbots that talk with children are under scrutiny. The design answers with adult control, a strict refusal to be a companion, speech turned into text at once and never kept, summaries rather than records, videos labelled as made by AI that show no real person and no child, a fence around a child's own future, and triage that stops an inquiry the moment a question is about the child's family, body, feelings or safety. Counsel has cleared a child speaking to the Primer, and the recording of children's speech with consent for testing, in the UK and the US, as the owner reported on 4 November 2026 (D118). That answers OI-17 for the Primer, and OI-13 only as it bears on the test recordings. The rest of OI-13, and OI-14 to OI-16, OI-18, OI-20 and OI-30 to OI-32, are open, and none is answered here.
