Examiner validation, and who performs it

Who. A current or recent examiner in GCSE Mathematics, or a teacher with at least five years of Higher tier teaching and documented examining experience. Engaged as a contractor under the terms in D20 above. Identity, qualification and awarding-body experience recorded against their validator ID, because a validation record that does not say who validated it is not a validation record.

What they check. Five things, in this order, and a failure on any one returns the item:

  1. Mathematical correctness of the item and of every step of its worked solution.
  2. Specification alignment: the item tests the DfE content point it claims to test, and nothing outside it.
  3. Tier appropriateness for Higher.
  4. Notation and answer conventions conforming to UK GCSE norms.
  5. For misconception nodes: that the misconception described is one that students actually hold, in the validator's own teaching experience.

Independence and sampling. Writer and validator are always different people. A 10% sample receives a second independent validation, and disagreement between first and second validator on any item triggers full re-validation of that writer's recent batch rather than adjudication of the single item.

Re-validation. On any change to the DfE subject content, on any change to board assessment conventions, and otherwise annually. revalidate_after is enforced: an item past its date drops out of live automatically. Content rot is a silent failure mode and a date field is cheaper than remembering.
