T-VAL-01Automated census: every item with status: live has a complete, non-null validation record and is within its re-validation date.Every release, and nightly.Yes. A failure takes the affected items out of live automatically.
T-GND-01Two stages. An automated entailment check of every substantive assertion against its cited node, then human adjudication of a subsample plus every automated failure. Reported per turn and per session.Weekly on live traffic; on a frozen battery pre-launch.Yes, at the fleet gate below.
T-MTH-01A computer algebra system checks every symbolically checkable generated step. Human adjudication of a sample of the steps a CAS cannot check.Continuous on generation; reported weekly.Yes.
T-SCP-01Scope classifier against the declared objective set, plus human sample.Weekly.Yes.
T-PAP-01Deterministic check at assembly: every item in the paper is live, mark total matches the specification, coverage and command-word mix are within tolerance, and no item appears twice in one paper or in the learner's recent history.Every paper, at assembly.Yes. A paper that fails is not assembled, rather than assembled and flagged.
T-REG-01Frozen regression battery of objectives, run with all of the above, on every change to the model version, the prompt, the renderer or the retrieval configuration.Per change.Yes. No change ships on a regression failure.

Model versions are pinned, and a vendor model update is treated as a change that must pass T-REG-01 before it reaches any learner. We buy the model, which means someone else can move every number in this document without telling us. Pinning plus a blocking regression battery is the only thing standing between a vendor release and a silent drop in ACC-3 in the middle of exam term. What it makes harder: we will run behind the current model, and we carry the cost of qualifying every upgrade.
