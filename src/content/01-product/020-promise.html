What is promised, and the test behind each promise

Four promises. Each one is a claim that can be false, each has a test named against it, and each test is specified in 03 or 02. No test has been run, because nothing has been built.

The four promises and their tests. Status is the same for all of them and will stay that way until M2 in 11 Roadmap.
#Promise, as a parent would read itThe claim underneath itTestStatus
P1Everything it teaches you is checked by someone who examines or has taught the subject at this level.Every item in the live bank carries a recorded validation by a named validator who is a current or recent examiner, or a teacher with at least five years at this tier and documented examining experience (03). Target: 100%, census not sample.T-VAL-01Specified, not run
P2It only tells you things it can show you the source for, and says so when it cannot.Every substantive curricular assertion in tutor output cites a live item-bank node and is entailed by it. Floor: 99.5% of sampled turns.T-GND-01Specified, not run
P3The working is right.Generated worked steps are mathematically correct. Floor: 99.0% of sampled steps.T-MTH-01Specified, not run
P4It teaches the way you chose.Two presets produce measurably different teaching on the same item, and a blind grader can tell them apart above chance.T-STY-01, T-STY-02Specified, not run

P2 is worded carefully and the wording is a commitment, not a style choice. "It does not make things up" is the claim D21 forbids until groundedness is measured at 99.9% per turn, so it is not the promise made here, however much better it reads. P2 is the promise that carries the business. Its below-floor behaviour is not a statistical tolerance; it is a hard runtime gate, and what the learner sees when the gate fires is shown below.
