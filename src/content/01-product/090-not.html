What the product does not do

Stated plainly, because each of these is a feature someone will ask for and each refusal is load-bearing somewhere else in this repository.

Does notWhy, and where the reasoning lives
Answer questions outside the declared launch scope.Correctness cannot be warranted outside the validated bank. It refuses rather than competing badly with free tools. 03
Reproduce, quote or paraphrase past papers, mark schemes or specification text.Clean-room content policy, D12. Verified AQA's published policy forbids use of its material in connection with AI tools. 04
Clone a creator's voice.D9. Highest-risk signal on both the rights and regulatory side, and the specific thing Ofcom named when warning about virtual clones. 04
Let a student create, name, customise or share a persona with another user.This is the exact trigger that converts the service into a user-to-user service under the Online Safety Act. It is an architectural invariant, not a product preference. 05
Behave as a friend or companion, claim feelings, or initiate contact.Safety, and it is what keeps the service out of the category Ofcom flagged. 05
Tell a student what grade they will get in the examination.The product shows a provisional grade range, which is a statement about current performance on validated items and explicitly not a forecast. The distinction is load-bearing, it is argued in 05, and the word prediction is not used anywhere until T-CAL-01 has calibration data.
Grade or mark a student's own written work, or anything they produce outside the item bank.Unwarrantable. Marking is only defensible against a mark scheme an examiner validated, and that exists only for our own items. 03
Write an essay, or produce work a student could submit.Academic-integrity exposure, and it is the use that makes schools refuse the product in Route B. 08
Offer counselling, or act as a place to disclose harm.We cannot staff it. It signposts and escalates instead; the honest limits are in 05.
