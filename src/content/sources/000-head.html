The design was written without web access, and the discipline throughout has been to use only what is in section 3, to mark everything else as assumption, and to refuse to fill gaps from memory. Where a figure has a natural decay rate, and several below do, the date it was checked is the important column.

What this register does not yet have, and a lawyer will ask for first. No URLs, no document versions, no clause or paragraph references. Counsel instructed on OI-1 will need the policy as published, with its version and date, not a quotation from a register.
