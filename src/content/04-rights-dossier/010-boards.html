The exam boards

The hardest constraint in the venture, and the one that dictates the content strategy in 03. Read the AQA wording carefully: on its face the second limb is broader than a training prohibition.

AQA

AQA's published policy states that AQA does not permit the use of any AQA material, in any manner or for any purpose, in connection with the training of AI-powered tools or technologies; and that it will not allow the use of any AQA material in or on any edition of a third-party work where any part of that work is generated or produced using AI.

VerifiedAQA published copyright and intellectual property policy, checked September 2026

Read plainly, the second limb bars an AI tutoring product from containing AQA material at all, not merely from training on it. That is a wider prohibition than the AI-training clauses most publishers have adopted, and it is the reason the clean-room policy is absolute rather than a training-time restriction.

Does AQA's clause reach runtime retrieval, that is retrieval-grounded use at inference, as well as training? The wording is broad enough to cover both and this has not been tested. Unblocks: whether any board-derived material could ever sit in a retrieval index, and therefore whether the clean room is a strategy or merely the current one. Cost of getting it wrong: a product built on a retrieval index that has to be destroyed, and a claim we would struggle to defend.

Pearson Edexcel

Pearson/Edexcel permits approved examination centres to reproduce past papers and mark schemes for practice, revision or teaching within the centre, on condition that students are not charged. A paid consumer product is outside that permission by construction.

Verifiedchecked September 2026

Note what this does and does not tell us. It is a permission granted to centres, not a general licence, and the no-charge condition excludes Route A by its own terms. It says nothing directly about AI. It is nonetheless a narrower and more legible constraint than AQA's, which is the basis of the provisional alignment decision D19 in 03.

OCR, WJEC and Eduqas

Whether OCR, WJEC and Eduqas take the same position as AQA has not been checked. It is assumed for planning that at least one of them has an AI clause of similar breadth, because AQA's is unlikely to be unique. That assumption has no evidential basis. Checking three published policies is a few hours of work and it is listed as a research task at M1 in 11 Roadmap, not as a counsel question, because it requires reading rather than advice.
