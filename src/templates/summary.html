AI.tutor

This vault is the design and planning record for an AI tutor for UK secondary students. A student chooses how they are taught by naming an educator whose style they already recognise, and nothing reaches that student which cannot be traced to an examiner-validated item.

Nothing described here has been built, and no test described here has been run; every accuracy and style claim in the vault is a commitment, not a measurement. This page is generated from the documents, the decision ledger, the open-items register and the dependency map. Every fact on it is transcluded from the page that states it, and every count is computed at build time, so nothing here can drift from the documents.
