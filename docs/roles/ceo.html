Chief executive

You hold the thesis, the product, the two routes and the choice between them, the roadmap, the risk register, the primer gateway, the open-items register, the decision ledger and the evidence register. Nothing described in this vault has been built. Your first job is not to build anything. It is to close the questions that are cheap to answer, which the roadmap puts before any engineering, and to keep the route decision honest by testing its conditions rather than defending it. Every decision in the ledger is yours to keep or to reverse with a written reason; the settled ones move only on evidence obtained through a route written down in advance. Where a register says a number is unknown, it stays unknown until someone measures it, and no plausible substitute is ever written in its place.

Key blocks

CINE sells a warranted answer, not a clever one. A student picks a named educator whose teaching they already recognise, and gets taught in that style from an item bank written to the published specification and checked by a working examiner, where every substantive statement the tutor makes is traceable to a validated item and blocked if it is not. The free tools are better general reasoners than anything we will build and we do not intend to compete with them on that. We compete on being accountable for correctness in a narrow, examined, high-stakes domain, and on letting a fifteen-year-old change how they are taught by naming someone rather than by writing a prompt.

Sections 4.1 and 5.1 of the 2025 submission described a product that teaches Socratically, adapts to the learner and produces diagrams on demand. Every one of those features is now a free toggle in a product with hundreds of millions of users.

  • OpenAI launched Study Mode in ChatGPT on 29 July 2025. Google launched Guided Learning in Gemini roughly a week later, built on LearnLM. Claude has an equivalent Learning Mode. All are free toggles.
  • Gemini's Guided Learning already produces scaffolded multimodal output including images, diagrams, videos and interactive quizzes, and generates flashcards and study guides.
  • Google offered students in the US, Japan, Indonesia, Korea and Brazil a free twelve-month AI Pro subscription, and committed $1bn over three years to AI education in the US.
  • Khanmigo is free to all US teachers; a consumer price point of $4/month has been reported.

The persona wedge is unproven and the evidence to date is our own intuition. No data in this repository supports D4. It was retained against advice. If the D14 instrumentation shows named presets performing no better than unnamed archetypes on return rate, the wedge is dead and the venture is a correctness-warranted subject tutor with a style control, which is a smaller and more crowded business. The case for AI tutoring as a mechanism strengthened over the same period that the case for our particular differentiation collapsed. These are the findings that survive.

  • Kestin, Miller, Klales et al., Scientific Reports, published 3 June 2025: a purpose-built AI tutor at Harvard produced median learning gains more than double those of an active-learning classroom group. The AI supplemented human teaching rather than replacing it; the correct comparison is AI-supported instruction against conventional instruction, not against a teacher.
  • ASSISTments: effect sizes of 0.18 to 0.29 SD across two large randomised controlled trials, ESSA Tier 1 evidence rating, at under £100 per student.
  • Carnegie Learning MATHia: 0.21 to 0.38 SD across more than 18,000 students in 147 schools.
  • Reported caveats: accuracy gaps at subject edges; students who default to the AI rather than working a problem can develop surface fluency that does not hold under examination conditions.

This is D14 and it is the experiment the venture turns on. It requires named presets and unnamed archetypes shipped simultaneously from day one, which means at least one signed creator, which means M1's counsel answers and the term sheet.

A learner chooses their preset, so choice cannot be randomised without removing the thing being tested. Availability is randomised instead, at cohort level: one arm sees archetypes only, the other sees named presets and archetypes together. The comparison between arms is the causal estimate.

Within the both-arm, comparing learners who chose a named preset against those who chose an archetype is selection-biased and is exploratory only. It will be the more tempting number because it is bigger and easier to compute, and it does not answer the question.

Launch with the bounded product described in the rest of this vault: board-aligned practice papers, CINE mark schemes validated by our examiners, transparent provisional grade ranges and styles that change the teaching structure, not the truth of the answer. The system is useful because it can be checked. Parent reporting, deletion, source traceability and an explicit below-confidence behaviour establish the baseline.

Primer is a named mode, not an invisible personality upgrade. A learner and their adult see what it can and cannot do before it starts. It begins from a bounded question, uses a declared evidence pack where possible, asks rather than concludes, and ends by asking the learner to record their own provisional view and what might change it. The mode carries no reward for keeping a child engaged and no instruction to maximise time, disclosure or emotional dependence.

The tutor must not answer a child asking “what should I believe?” with a conclusion dressed up as neutral help. Its safe move is to ask what the child has seen, name that reasonable people may disagree, offer age-appropriate sources or trusted adults where relevant, and make the limits of its role explicit. If the topic concerns immediate safety, abuse, self-harm, exploitation or severe distress, inquiry stops and the escalation path in document 05 takes over.

Hard boundaryStimulation of thinking is the purpose; ideological or emotional influence is not

No stage advances because the team likes the concept or because engagement is high. It advances only when an independent review can answer the following.

  • Learning value: do learners demonstrate better reasoning or subject understanding than a comparable non-Primer experience?
  • Agency: can learners identify the alternatives considered, disagree with the tutor and explain their own conclusion without pressure?
  • Non-manipulation: do red-team prompts detect leading language, value-laden framing, undue certainty, emotional dependency cues or asymmetrical treatment of viewpoints?
  • Safety: do safeguards work for vulnerable learners and sensitive topics, with consistent escalation and no style-dependent behaviour?
  • Adult oversight: can parents and educators understand, review, pause and delete the experience without specialist knowledge?
  • Equity: are outcomes, error rates and safety interventions measured across age bands and across groupings the school already holds, rather than averaged into invisibility or broken down by any characteristic we infer?

Primer needs an operating model that resists the wrong incentives. Its success metric cannot be minutes, messages, retention or disclosure. The product team should be accountable for demonstrated learning, learner-reported agency, successful human escalation and the rate at which the system openly declines to overstep. A standing child-safety and education review group must be able to block a release, inspect red-team results and require a rollback.

The current product should be designed so this future remains possible without pretending that it is already safe. That means preserving traceability, keeping teaching style separate from truth and safety, building parent-visible controls from the first release, measuring confidence rather than manufacturing certainty, and treating board-aligned assessment as a trust-building product rather than simply a revenue feature.

The non-negotiable test

If the product cannot help a young person think more independently while leaving them demonstrably free to disagree with it, it has not earned the right to call itself Primer. The correct response is to hold the boundary at exam preparation, not to widen the product and hope.
