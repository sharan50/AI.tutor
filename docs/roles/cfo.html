Chief financial officer

You own the economics: cost per session built up from the architecture, the variable that dominates and why it is derived rather than assumed, the acquisition ceiling set by the examination calendar, sensitivity, examiner validation as a line item, price points, and the order in which the unknowns must be measured. You also own the pricing and unit economics sections of both routes. Nothing has been built and no price has been tested; the ledger records price as deliberately not decided. Every figure you inherit is from the evidence register, a stated design choice, or marked unknown, and the discipline you hold is that no fourth kind ever appears.

          Unknownrequired inputs

          A financial model is the easiest place in a venture document to put a number that nobody checks and everybody later repeats. Every quantity below is one of exactly three things: a figure from the verified findings, a design choice made in this repository, or an Unknown that is named and left empty. There is no fourth category and there are no illustrative figures, because illustrative figures get quoted.

          Two structural properties follow from the architecture and hold whatever the vendor prices turn out to be. First, cost per turn is approximately constant rather than growing with session length, because D30 bounds every term in the context. Second, the struggling learner is expensive linearly, not quadratically, which matters because the struggling learner is the one the product exists for and is the one an unbounded-context design would have made uneconomic.

          The dominant quantity is the payback ratio

          R = (CAC + V) / ((p - c) x m)          viable iff R < 1

          and it dominates for a structural reason rather than a numerical one: its denominator is capped by external facts while its numerator has no anchor at all. Price is anchored by the verified tutoring rate, variable cost is bounded by the architecture, and retained months are capped by the examination calendar. The whole business therefore reduces to a single question, which is whether acquisition plus verification can be brought in under a ceiling that somebody else has already set.

          The finding14 September 2026

          This is why condition C2 is a kill condition rather than a cost line. V is a one-off subtracted from a capped denominator, so every pound of verification cost removes a pound from the acquisition budget directly. A large verification quote does not make Route A more expensive; it makes it arithmetically impossible, and no amount of product work recovers it.

          The operational consequence is in 07: acquire Year 10 deliberately in the autumn, accept a worse conversion rate for the better denominator, and report the two cohorts separately from the first customer rather than adopting the split later when the blended number becomes misleading.

          It is a fixed cost, not a variable one, and putting it in the right place changes what it means. Content cost does not scale with learners; it scales with items, once. So it does not appear in c and does not affect R. It sets the number of households the business must reach before it covers its content base.

          Both unknowns are specified in 03, along with the timed pilot that resolves them. minutes_per_item is the more important of the two, and not because of the launch bill. It is the constant that determines whether the content moat extends to a second strand, a second subject and a second board. If it is small, the moat compounds; if it is large, the content strategy is a one-strand business wearing the language of a platform, and the defensibility argument in 00 does not survive contact with a spreadsheet.

          The addition this document makes is the reminder that price is the narrowest of the five terms, bounded above by a verified comparator and below by free tools. Effort spent optimising price is effort spent on the term with the least room in it. The same effort spent on CAC + V, or on shifting acquisition toward Year 10, moves R far more.

          That parents anchor against tutoring rates rather than against software prices or against free.
