AI.tutor

This vault is the design and planning record for an AI tutor for UK secondary students. A student chooses how they are taught by naming an educator whose style they already recognise, and nothing reaches that student which cannot be traced to an examiner-validated item.

Nothing described here has been built, and no test described here has been run; every accuracy and style claim in the vault is a commitment, not a measurement. This page is generated from the documents, the decision ledger, the open-items register and the dependency map. Every fact on it is transcluded from the page that states it, and every count is computed at build time, so nothing here can drift from the documents. Read the route first, then the door for the domain you own, then the contents page for the four reader routes.

The route

Route A, direct to parents. The verified demand data points that way and the verified school data points away from Route B. But the recommendation holds only if three conditions are met, all three are testable before anything is built, and failing any one of them should flip the decision rather than start an argument about it.

What is settled

  • D1 UK first. Bengaluru is the build base; the UK is the market.
  • D2 Two routes planned in parallel. Route A is the current preference.
  • D3 Shared core written once; divergence confined to pricing, data and consent, distribution and compliance.
  • D4 Named-educator personas are the wedge, retained against advice.
  • D5 Personas pass a student usefulness test, not a fan's ear test. Name and likeness only; no creator material ingested.
  • D6 The route to high-fidelity personas via exemplar conditioning must be designed now, not bolted on later.
  • D7 Style is a structured parameter object, with a written style card generated from it.
  • D8 No persona LoRAs or fine-tuned weights carrying creator style. Retrieval and prompting only.
  • D9 Voice cloning is off the launch path; a separate opt-in limb of the licence.
  • D10 Student-facing style control: a creator selects a preset, the student may move individual dimensions.
  • D11 Zero-persona operation is a hard requirement. The product launches and runs with no signed creators.
  • D12 Clean-room content. Original items written to the published specification, examiner-validated. No exam board material enters the system.
  • D13 No offshore litigation-evasion strategy. The Indian company is retained for ordinary tax and capital reasons.
  • D14 Ship named presets and unnamed archetypes simultaneously from day one; instrument return rate, not satisfaction.

The numbers that matter

Fifteen minutes to disabled, seven days to deleted, thirty-five days to gone from backups. Measured from receipt of a valid notice at a published address, not from when someone reads it.

A 99.5% per-turn floor sounds strong. Per session it is not, and the honest number is the one a parent experiences.

The dominant quantity is the payback ratio

R = (CAC + V) / ((p - c) x m)          viable iff R < 1

and it dominates for a structural reason rather than a numerical one: its denominator is capped by external facts while its numerator has no anchor at all. Price is anchored by the verified tutoring rate, variable cost is bounded by the architecture, and retained months are capped by the examination calendar. The whole business therefore reduces to a single question, which is whether acquisition plus verification can be brought in under a ceiling that somebody else has already set.

What is not known

  • Open OI-1 Does AQA's AI clause reach runtime retrieval, or only training?
  • Open OI-4 Is a one-to-one tutor with no inter-user sharing outside the Online Safety Act user-to-user duties, and precisely which feature changes that?
  • Open OI-5 What age assurance method would satisfy the ICO for a service whose entire user base is children, at a cost compatible with a consumer price point?
  • Open OI-7 Which entity contracts with the parent, and what does that do to consumer jurisdiction, UK representative obligations and VAT?
  • Assumed OA-10 New, and the most load-bearing assumption in the repository. That parents anchor an AI tutoring subscription against the hourly cost of a human tutor rather than against software prices, or against free.
  • Assumed OA-15 New. That variable cost per month is small relative to price, which is what makes engineering cost reduction low-leverage in 10.
