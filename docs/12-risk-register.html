The ordering is recorded as the owner gave it, and it is defensible as a ranking of what is existential and outside his control: rights are negotiations with counterparties who can refuse, whereas data protection is a discipline that can be executed. The engineering standard does not follow that ordering. Item 4 is built to the top standard because it is cheap to design in, ruinous to retrofit, and because in the chosen market the regulator with an active enforcement programme aimed at this sector governs exactly it.
