Reasoned but not verified

  • That Save My Exams, Physics & Maths Tutor, Seneca, Cognito and Twinkl sell board-aligned material without licensing past papers. Inferred from their existence, not from their terms. This is the cheapest available de-risking of the entire plan and should be done first.
  • That UK persona protection runs through passing off for false endorsement, trade marks, copyright in the underlying videos, performers' rights and data protection, there being no statutory UK personality right.
  • That UK GDPR Article 3(2) and the Article 27 representative requirement apply to an India-established controller serving UK children.
  • That most creators worth signing are US-resident, bringing US state right-of-publicity law into scope.
  • That the seven style dimensions in document 02 are independent and complete. No teacher has reviewed them.
  • That a 60-minute kill switch is achievable. Promised in document 04, unmeasured in document 06.
