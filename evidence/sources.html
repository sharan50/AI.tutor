Evidence on AI tutoring

  • Kestin, Miller, Klales et al., Scientific Reports, 3 June 2025. Harvard RCT; AI tutor produced median learning gains more than double the active-learning classroom group.
  • ASSISTments: effect sizes 0.18–0.29 SD across two large RCTs, ESSA Tier 1 rating, under £100 per student.
  • Carnegie Learning MATHia: 0.21–0.38 SD across 18,000+ students in 147 schools.

Free competition

  • OpenAI Study Mode, launched 29 July 2025.
  • Google Guided Learning in Gemini, launched c. 6 August 2025, built on LearnLM. Free 12-month AI Pro for students in the US, Japan, Indonesia, Korea and Brazil; $1bn over three years committed to US AI education.
  • Khanmigo free to all US teachers; $4/month consumer price point reported.
  • UK: Third Space Learning (Skye), Century Tech, Oak National Academy (Aila).

UK market

  • Sutton Trust, Private Tutoring 2026, published February 2026. 29% of secondary pupils in England and Wales ever tutored (18% twenty years ago, 27% in 2019); Year 11 25%, Year 10 10%; London 45%, rest of England 27%, Wales 24%; urban 33%, rural 19%; worst-off households 23%, best-off 30%. School-organised tutoring 20% of pupils, down from 22% in 2023; 58% of schools reduced their tutoring offer year on year.
  • Prevailing 2026 rates: c. £25–£45/hr GCSE, £35–£65/hr A-level. Earlier Sutton Trust estimate of the UK private tuition market: up to £2bn.

Exam board rights

  • AQA copyright and intellectual property policy, aqa.org.uk. Does not permit use of AQA material in any manner or for any purpose in connection with training AI-powered tools; will not allow AQA material in any third-party work where any part is AI-generated.
  • Pearson copyright policy, qualifications.pearson.com. Approved centres may reproduce past papers and mark schemes for practice, revision or teaching within the centre, provided students are not charged.

UK regulators

  • ICO fine, Reddit, £14.47m, 24 February 2026: inadequate age assurance, profiling children without lawful basis. MediaLab fined February 2026. Prior: TikTok £12.7m, Snap £1.95m.
  • ICO open letter, 12 March 2026: self-declared age inadequate where a service is likely to be accessed by children. ICO/Ofcom joint statement on age assurance, March 2026. ICO has signalled edtech audits.
  • ICO maximum penalty: higher of £17.5m or 4% of global annual turnover.
  • Ofcom open letter to service providers, 8 November 2024: OSA reaches generative AI; a service is user-to-user where users can share chatbot output with others or create chatbots available to others.
  • Ofcom penalties: higher of £18m or 10% of qualifying worldwide revenue. Business disruption and service restriction orders may require ISPs to block, app stores to remove, payment providers to cease processing.
  • Ofcom FOI response, 18 August 2026: penalties totalling £6.1m imposed on nine companies not UK-based or not stating jurisdiction, 1 April 2025 to 30 June 2026.

India

Section 9(3) prohibits tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising at children, not waivable by consent. Fourth Schedule Part A Entry 3 exempts educational institutions and fiduciaries engaged by them, purpose-limited. Children's obligations effective c. 18 months from notification (c. May 2027).
  • IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules 2026, G.S.R. 120(E), notified 10 February 2026, in force 20 February 2026: synthetically generated information defined; prominent labelling and unstrippable provenance metadata; three-hour takedown for flagged unlawful content, two hours for non-consensual sexual imagery.
