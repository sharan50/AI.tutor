These findings were checked in September 2026 against the sources named, and are reproduced here from BUILD_BRIEF.md section 3, which is the source instruction for this repository.

This repository has not independently re-verified them. The design was written without web access, and the discipline throughout has been to use only what is in section 3, to mark everything else as assumption, and to refuse to fill gaps from memory. So the correct reading of a Verified marker is "checked in September 2026 by the owner against the named source", not "checked twice".

Where a figure has a natural decay rate, and several below do, the date it was checked is the important column.

What this register does not yet have, and a lawyer will ask for first. No URLs, no document versions, no clause or paragraph references. EV-21 is the clearest case: the AQA wording on which the entire content strategy rests is sourced as "AQA published copyright and IP policy", which is enough to plan against and not enough to advise against. Counsel instructed on OI-1 will need the policy as published, with its version and date, not a quotation from a register.

Adding locators is a named task at M1, alongside the other reading tasks. Until it is done, every row here should be read as "this is what we believe the source says", and no row should be quoted to a third party as though it had been verified from the primary document by the person quoting it.

4. Exam board rights

#ClaimSourceCheckedUsed in
EV-21AQA's published copyright and intellectual property policy states that AQA does not permit the use of any AQA material, in any manner or for any purpose, in connection with the training of AI-powered tools or technologies; and that it will not allow the use of any AQA material in or on any edition of a third-party work where any part of that work is generated or produced using AI. Read plainly, the second limb bars an AI tutoring product from containing AQA material at all, not merely from training on it.AQA published copyright and IP policySept 202603, 04
EV-22Pearson/Edexcel permits approved examination centres to reproduce past papers and mark schemes for practice, revision or teaching within the centre, on condition that students are not charged. A paid consumer product is outside that permission by construction.Pearson published termsSept 202603, 04
EV-23Both boards assert copyright over specifications, schemes of work, past papers and mark schemes.Published policiesSept 202604
EV-24Copyright protects expression, not facts or curricula. GCSE and A-level subject content is set and published by the DfE.DfE published subject contentSept 202603, 04

What is deliberately absent from this register

AbsentWhy
Model vendor prices, context limits and capability benchmarks.Not in the brief's verified findings and not written from memory. They change fast enough that a figure written today would be wrong before the item bank exists. Gathered at M2; see 06.
