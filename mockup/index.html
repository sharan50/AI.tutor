Primer demo: the design mock-up

This is how the demonstration in the MVP build guide is meant to look and behave once it is built. Nothing here is built. No AI runs: the answers and pictures are written examples, the microphone and the passcode are simulated, and the timings are design targets, not measurements.

Drawn on 25 October 2026 and reviewed by the owner, who removed one control (D75). Published here on 26 October 2026, with its words brought into line with the specification, which governs where the two differ. The replies to sensitive questions are drafts the owner has still to approve.

Try it

Enter any passcode and press Start. Then ask out loud, type a question or tap an example. Two questions have written answers, why the sky is blue and why you cannot divide by zero. A question about someone's own life, a death or a big subject such as religion gets a careful reply instead.

Before you start

For adults only

You play a child aged 8 to 13. Please don't let a real child use this demo.

Where your words go

Your questions go to Anthropic, whose AI writes the answers. When you speak, your recording goes to Google to be turned into text. This demo keeps no recordings and no record of who asked what.

Nothing is checked yet

Answers and videos are made by AI for each question. Nobody checks them first, so they can be wrong.

Hard questions get careful replies

Questions about your own life, or big subjects like death or religion, get a reply written in advance, and no video.

The sky question on a phone, at the times the design aims for. How long a video really takes is unknown until the first clips are made on the first day of the build (OA-41).

Safety and failure

A sensitive question gets a reply written in advance, and no video. A video that fails twice becomes a still picture, and its block can still be heard.
