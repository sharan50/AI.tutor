# Creator licence: term sheet

**CINE · style preset licence · name and likeness only**
Version 1.0, 14 September 2026

> This is a plain-English summary written for you, not for a lawyer. It is not
> the agreement and it is not legal advice; it describes what the agreement will
> say. The drafting version is `creator-licence-heads-of-terms.md` in this same
> folder, and you should have your own adviser read it. **No lawyer has yet
> reviewed either document.**

---

## What we are asking for

Your **name** and your **face**, used to label one teaching style inside our
tutoring product for UK students.

That is the whole of it.

## What we are not asking for

This list matters more than the one above, so it is longer.

- **None of your content.** Not your videos, not your transcripts, not your
  slides, not your worked examples, not your notes. Nothing you have made goes
  into our system, our database, or our AI model.
- **Not your voice.** No voice cloning, no synthesised speech, nothing that
  sounds like you. This is a separate agreement we are not asking you to sign.
- **Not exclusivity.** You can do the same deal with anyone else, including a
  direct competitor of ours, on the same day. We are not asking you to choose us.
- **Not your time.** Beyond one review step described below, we are not asking
  you to record anything, appear in anything, or make anything.
- **Not advertising.** We will not put your name in a paid advertising campaign
  without asking you first, separately, every time.
- **Not merchandise.** Nothing physical, ever.
- **Not your endorsement of what the tutor says.** The product will state on
  screen that you do not write or check its answers, because you do not.

## How your teaching style is captured, given that we take none of your content

Three qualified teachers watch your **publicly available** videos and score your
teaching against a published rubric of twelve things, such as whether you start
from a concrete example or from the rule, how long you wait before stating the
general case, and whether you correct a mistake directly or make the student
spot it.

The result is **twelve numbers**. That is what we store. We do not download,
transcribe, keep or feed in any of your material; the raters watch it the way
anyone watches it, and write down scores.

**You see those twelve numbers, and the written instruction generated from them,
before anything goes live, and you can object to any of it.** That is a term of
the agreement, not a favour. You can ask to review it again once a year.

## What appears on screen

Every session, permanently visible, not hidden in a menu:

> AI tutor. Style preset licensed from **[your name]**. **[Your name]** does not
> write or check these answers.

If a student adjusts the style away from your settings, the label changes to say
so, because you should not be shown as the author of something a student made up.

We cannot change that wording without asking you.

## You can check on it, at any time

You can ask us, whenever you like, for a sample of real sessions running under your
preset, so you can see what it actually does rather than take our word for it. If
you see something you do not want associated with you, tell us and we remove that
behaviour **within 14 days**. You do not have to terminate to get something changed.

## How long, and how you get out

- **Term:** twelve months, then rolling twelve months unless either of us gives
  notice.
- **Ending it:** you can end this **at any time, for any reason or for no
  reason, with no notice period and no cure period.** You do not have to tell us
  why. You do not have to be in dispute with us.

## What happens when you end it

This is the part to check, because it is the part most companies are vague about.
Measured from when your notice reaches us, not from when we read it:

| Time | What has happened |
|---|---|
| **15 minutes** | Your preset is switched off for every new session. |
| **16 minutes** | Any session still running switches to an unnamed style. |
| **24 hours** | Your name and image are gone from the catalogue, our website, and app store listings. |
| **7 days** | Your twelve numbers, the generated instruction, your name and your images are deleted from our systems. |
| **35 days** | Our backups have aged out. |

**Why we can promise fifteen minutes:** there is no AI model trained on you. Your
style is twelve numbers in a database, and switching you off is changing one
value. Nothing has to be retrained, because nothing was trained. This is a
deliberate design decision on our side and it is the reason this promise is worth
anything.

**The honest caveat:** past sessions students have already had are kept for those
students, because they are the student's own record. Your name is stripped from
them. We are telling you this rather than leaving it out.

## Payment

Not settled. Either a flat fee for the term or a share of revenue from sessions
using your preset. We have deliberately not written a number here, because we do
not have one and we would rather say so.

## If we ever want more than this

Two things would need a **new, separate agreement** that you would be free to
refuse without affecting this one:

1. **Using your actual material** to make the style closer to yours. That is a
   content licence, it is a different negotiation, and the fifteen-minute
   deletion promise above gets slower and more complicated if we ever do it, though
   the fifteen-minute switch-off does not change. We would tell you exactly how much
   slower before asking.
2. **Your voice.** Separate agreement, separate payment, and we are not asking.

## Questions you should ask us

Put here because you should ask them, and because we would rather you asked us
than found out later.

1. Who are the three raters, and what are they qualified in?
2. Can I see the twelve numbers and the rubric before I sign, not just before
   launch?
3. What happens if a student complains that the tutor taught them something wrong
   while it had my name on it?
4. Where is the company, and which country's courts would we be in if this went
   wrong?
5. Has anyone checked my public videos are the ones you are scoring, and can I
   tell you which ones are representative?

Question 3 has a real answer and it is in `docs/03-curriculum-and-content.html`:
the tutor's content comes from a separate bank of material checked by
examiners, your preset changes only the teaching style and never the facts, and
there is an automatic test that enforces that separation. Question 4 is not
settled and we will tell you when it is.
